Learning outcomes

Objectives, COs and programme mapping

Official course objectives

  1. To familiarize the language of graphics used to express ideas and convey instructions while carrying out engineering jobs.
  2. To familiarize drafting and sketching skills, applications of drawing equipment and Indian Standards related to engineering drawings and dimensioning styles.
  3. To provide skills to translate ideas into sketches and draw and read engineering curves, conic sections and generation of 3D solid surfaces into plane areas.
  4. To apply basic skills to develop projection of points and lines.
  5. To familiarize the knowledge of orthographic views and sectional views of objects in engineering applications.

Module 1

Introduction

This module establishes the visual grammar of engineering drawings: terminology, standards, dimensioning and regular-figure construction.

9 Lofficial hours
CO1mapped CO

Learning goals

  • Identify instruments, sheet layouts and line types.
  • Apply dimensioning standards.
  • Construct four regular polygons.

Key facts

  • Use light construction lines before final outlines.
  • Title blocks identify drawing information.
  • Dimensions must be unambiguous.
  • Polygon accuracy depends on equal divisions.
Engineering relevance: Clear graphics reduce manufacturing and inspection errors.
Basic terminology of engineering graphics and standards

Engineering graphics is a visual language. Learn drawing instruments, sheet sizes, board types, layout, title block, line types and the role of Indian Standards in clear technical communication.

Malayalam support: Drawing language ഒരു common technical language ആണ്. Line type ഉം thickness ഉം meaning മാറ്റുന്നു.
Practice: Prepare a title block and a line-type strip before beginning construction exercises.
Principles of dimensioning

Dimensioning communicates the size and location of features without ambiguity. Use correct extension lines, dimension lines, arrowheads, numerical values, units and spacing according to the prescribed standards. Scales are for study purpose only in this source.

  1. Choose a suitable scale when required.
  2. Place dimensions outside the object where possible.
  3. Avoid duplicated dimensions.
  4. Keep figures legible and aligned.
Malayalam support: Dimensioning എന്നത് object-ന്റെ size/location വ്യക്തമാക്കുന്ന രീതിയാണ്. Same dimension രണ്ടുതവണ നൽകരുത്.
Regular polygons

Construct a pentagon, hexagon, heptagon and octagon by a suitable method when the side length is given. Begin with a clean baseline, establish the required centres or arcs, and retain construction lines lightly until verification.

Module summary

A neat drawing is not decoration; it is technical communication.

Module 2

Basic Figures

Conics, miscellaneous curves and surface development extend drawing from basic figures to engineering forms.

12 Lofficial hours
CO2mapped CO

Learning goals

  • Construct ellipse and parabola by specified methods.
  • Draw involutes.
  • Develop prism, cylinder and 90° elbow surfaces.

Key facts

  • Only the prescribed methods are examined.
  • Smooth curve joining matters.
  • Development preserves true surface lengths.
  • The elbow is two-piece symmetric.
Engineering relevance: Patterns derived from developments support sheet-metal and fabrication work.
Conic sections

Study the terminology and construction of ellipse and parabola. For an ellipse use the rectangular method and concentric-circle method only. For a parabola use the tangent method only, as specified.

  1. Set the reference axes and required dimensions.
  2. Construct the control geometry.
  3. Generate points using the prescribed method.
  4. Join points with a smooth continuous curve.
Malayalam support: Conic construction-ൽ method മാറി ഉപയോഗിക്കരുത്; syllabus പറഞ്ഞ method മാത്രം practice ചെയ്യുക.
Miscellaneous curves

Construct the involute of a circle and the involute of a rectangle. An involute is generated by unwinding a taut string from the boundary; accurate division and tangent-like arcs are important.

Malayalam support: Involute ഒരു boundary-ൽ നിന്ന് string unwind ചെയ്യുന്നതായി visualise ചെയ്യാം. Division points ശരിയായി mark ചെയ്യുക.
Common mistake: Joining points with straight segments instead of a smooth curve.
Development of surfaces

Develop the surfaces of a rectangular prism, cylinder and a 90° elbow. The elbow is a two-piece symmetric type with standard side outlets and equal cross-sectional areas. Development converts a three-dimensional surface into a plane pattern for fabrication.

Development task

Rectangular prismFlat faces laid out with shared edges
CylinderRectangular stretch-out with circular ends

Module summary

Method selection and construction sequence are the core skills.

Module 3

Basic Projections

Projection theory explains how three-dimensional positions become readable two-dimensional views.

9 Lofficial hours
CO3mapped CO

Learning goals

  • Distinguish first and third angle projection.
  • Project points in four quadrants.
  • Project first-quadrant lines under stated limits.

Key facts

  • XY is the reference line.
  • Quadrant changes view positions.
  • Projectors are perpendicular to reference planes.
  • Keep labels and construction geometry organised.
Engineering relevance: Projection is used in design documentation, inspection and manufacturing communication.
Basic projections and projection theory

Outline first-angle and third-angle projection theory. Understand the reference planes and the relative placement of views before constructing projections.

Malayalam support: First angle, third angle methods-ൽ views എവിടെ വരും എന്ന് ആദ്യം പഠിക്കുക; projection symbol/arrangement ശ്രദ്ധിക്കുക.
Projections of points

Project points in all four quadrants. The location of the point relative to horizontal and vertical planes determines the relative position of its front and top views.

  1. Draw the XY reference line.
  2. Locate the point in the specified quadrant.
  3. Project perpendiculars to the reference planes.
  4. Label the front/top view consistently.
Malayalam support: Four quadrants-ൽ point position മാറുമ്പോൾ front/top view position മാറും.
Projections of lines

Develop projections of lines in the first quadrant only. Questions may be limited to lines inclined to any one plane only, including lines parallel to both planes, parallel to one and perpendicular to the other, and parallel to one and inclined to the other.

Malayalam support: Line projection-ൽ true length, inclination, and projector direction വേർതിരിച്ച് രേഖപ്പെടുത്തുക.

Module summary

Always establish the reference plane before transferring a view.

Module 4

Projections — 3D

This module converts pictorial objects into orthographic and full sectional views.

15 Lofficial hours
CO4mapped CO

Learning goals

  • Read simple isometric figures.
  • Construct first-angle orthographic views.
  • Construct full sectional views.

Key facts

  • Front view choice controls the remaining views.
  • Isometric axes are equally inclined in the drawing convention.
  • Section hatching identifies cut material.
  • Alignment prevents view mismatch.
Engineering relevance: Manufacturing drawings and inspection plans depend on coordinated views and sections.
Projections of objects: orthographic and isometric

Orthographic projection represents an object with separate principal views; isometric projection gives a pictorial three-dimensional representation. Practise simple pictorial isometric drawings and identify front, top and side views.

Malayalam support: Isometric view object-നെ 3D ആയി കാണിക്കുന്നു; orthographic views exact front/top/side information നൽകുന്നു.
Orthographic projections

Construct orthographic projections of simple engineering objects from pictorial views using the first-angle projection method only. Align projectors carefully and maintain correspondence between views.

  1. Select the front view.
  2. Project top and side views using construction lines.
  3. Transfer depths consistently.
  4. Darken visible outlines and add required dimensions.
Sectional views of objects

Construct full sectional views of simple engineering objects. A cutting plane exposes internal details; hatch the cut surfaces uniformly and omit hidden lines where the section convention requires.

Malayalam support: Sectional view internal shape കാണിക്കാൻ cutting plane ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നു. Hatch lines uniform spacing-ൽ വരയ്ക്കുക.
Drawing-room safety: Handle compass points, dividers and mini-drafters carefully; store instruments safely after use.

Module summary

Use a repeatable view-projection checklist rather than guessing placements.

Practice

Module-wise questions and answers

Module 1 questions

What is the purpose of a title block?
It records drawing identification such as title, scale, date and drafter information.
Name two dimensioning elements.
Dimension line and extension line are two elements.
How many sides does a heptagon have?
Seven.

Module 2 questions

Name the two ellipse methods prescribed.
Rectangular method and concentric-circle method.
What is surface development?
It is the unfolding of a three-dimensional surface into a plane pattern.
What is an involute?
A curve generated by unwinding a taut string from a curve or boundary.

Module 3 questions

What is first-angle projection?
A projection convention in which the object is considered between observer and plane, producing prescribed view placement.
Why use projectors?
To transfer the position of a point or feature between reference planes.
Which quadrant is used for line questions?
First quadrant only, within the stated syllabus limits.

Module 4 questions

What is orthographic projection?
A set of principal views projected perpendicular to reference planes.
What is a sectional view?
A view obtained by imagining an object cut to expose internal details.
Why hatch a section?
To indicate the surfaces cut by the cutting plane.

Quick revision and exam checklist

Drawing checklist

  • Identify the prescribed method before starting.
  • Use correct line types and light construction geometry.
  • Keep views aligned and labelled.
  • Use first-angle convention where specified.
  • Hatch full sections uniformly and present a clean final outline.
