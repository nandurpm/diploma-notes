Learning outcomes

Objectives, COs and programme mapping

Official course objectives

  1. To familiarize the language of graphics used to express ideas and convey instructions while carrying out engineering jobs.
  2. To provide skills to translate ideas into sketches and draw/read engineering curves, conic sections and generate 3D solid surfaces into plane areas.
  3. To apply basic skill to develop projection of points, lines, orthographic views and sectional views of objects.
  4. To familiarize the use of CAD software.
  5. To acquire skill in developing orthographic and sectional views using CAD.

Module 1

Geometrical construction and surface development

Module I develops polygon, conic and surface-development skills that connect manual construction to engineering fabrication.

9official hours
CO1mapped CO

Learning goals

  • Construct regular polygons.
  • Construct ellipse by two methods.
  • Develop a prism and cylinder surface.

Key facts

  • Side length controls polygon construction.
  • Ellipse construction must be smooth.
  • Development preserves edges and lengths.
  • Keep construction lines light.
Engineering relevance: Sheet-metal patterns and design documentation depend on accurate developments.
Drawing regular polygons

Construct pentagon and hexagon by any suitable method when the side length is given. The self-learning extension includes pentagon, hexagon, heptagon and octagon terminology and construction.

  1. Set the side length and baseline.
  2. Construct the required arcs or divisions.
  3. Locate all vertices.
  4. Join and darken the final polygon.
Malayalam support: Side length given ആണെങ്കിൽ construction geometry ആദ്യം light line ആയി വരയ്ക്കുക; final outline അവസാനം darken ചെയ്യുക.
Drawing conic sections

Construct an ellipse by the rectangular method and concentric-circle method. The activity also includes parabola and hyperbola construction for extended practice, while the prescribed practical experiment specifically names ellipse methods.

Malayalam support: Ellipse-ന്റെ രണ്ട് prescribed methods വേർതിരിച്ച് practice ചെയ്യുക. Curve smooth ആയി join ചെയ്യുക.
Drawing development of surfaces

Develop a rectangular prism and cylinder. The activity introduces surface-development procedures for engineering applications; measure shared edges carefully so the pattern can be folded or rolled.

Malayalam support: Development എന്നത് 3D surface-നെ plane pattern ആക്കുന്നതാണ്. Shared edges mismatch ആകരുത്.

Module summary

Construct cleanly, label method, and retain a record of the sequence.

Module 2

Projection, orthographic and sectional views

Module II converts spatial positions and isometric figures into standard engineering views.

15official hours
CO2mapped CO

Learning goals

  • Use first/third-angle theory.
  • Project points and first-quadrant lines.
  • Create orthographic and sectional views.

Key facts

  • Quadrants determine view positions.
  • Orthographic views must align.
  • Minimum practical exercises are specified.
  • Section hatching indicates cut surfaces.
Engineering relevance: Engineering drawings communicate form to manufacturing and inspection teams.
Drawing basic projections

Outline first-angle and third-angle projection theory. Projection conventions determine where the related views are placed and must be understood before drawing.

Malayalam support: First angle, third angle projection-ൽ object/plane relationshipയും view placementഉം മനസ്സിലാക്കുക.
Projections of points and lines

Develop projections of points in all four quadrants and projections of lines in the first quadrant only. Construct projectors accurately and keep the reference line visible until the views are checked.

  1. Draw XY reference.
  2. Locate the point or line position.
  3. Project to the relevant plane.
  4. Label and compare the resulting views.
Orthographic projections and sectional views of objects

Develop orthographic and sectional views of simple engineering objects from isometric figures. The practical requirement is a minimum of three orthographic exercises and two sectional exercises.

Malayalam support: Isometric figure നോക്കി front/top/side views derive ചെയ്യുക. Sectional view-ൽ cut surfaces hatch ചെയ്യുക.

Module summary

Start from the reference line and maintain projection discipline.

Module 3

CAD software and basic commands

Module III introduces the digital drafting environment and the commands needed to create, edit and annotate geometry.

9official hours
CO3mapped CO

Learning goals

  • Open and save drawings.
  • Configure units, limits, grid, snap and coordinates.
  • Use draw and modify commands.
  • Apply properties, dimensions and text.

Key facts

  • Save often and use meaningful filenames.
  • Object selection controls command behaviour.
  • Line properties affect readability.
  • Hatch is both graphical and semantic.
Engineering relevance: CAD improves repeatability, accuracy and editing efficiency compared with paper-only drafting.
CAD software introduction and basic commands

Familiarise yourself with CAD application, advantages over manual drafting, hardware and software requirements, new/open/save/save as/exit, drawing editor, mouse and pull-down commands, help, data entry and entity selection.

Malayalam support: CAD software-ൽ file save ചെയ്യുന്നത് പതിവാക്കുക. New drawing, units, limits, grid, snap, osnap, coordinates, ortho mode എന്നിവ setup-ന്റെ ഭാഗമാണ്.
Drawing commands

Use line, circle, arc, ellipse, rectangle, polygon, spline, polyline and hatch. Establish limits, units, grid, snap, object snap, coordinates and ortho mode before creating geometry.

Workflow tip: Setup → draw basic geometry → inspect object selection → save a named version before modification.
Modify commands, properties, dimensioning and text

Practise erase, copy, array, rotate, mirror, offset, scale, move, trim, fillet, chamfer, extend, stretch, polyline edit and explode. Work with line colour, line weight, line type, dimensions and text.

Malayalam support: Modify commands ആവർത്തിച്ച് practice ചെയ്താൽ manual redrawing കുറയും. Properties uniform ആക്കി dimensions/text readable ആക്കുക.

Module summary

Build a command habit: setup, draw, modify, annotate, save.

Module 4

Orthographic and sectional development in CAD

Module IV applies the CAD command set to complete orthographic and sectional drawing exercises.

9official hours
CO4mapped CO

Learning goals

  • Create at least three orthographic views.
  • Create at least two sectional views.
  • Add dimensions and text.
  • Prepare fair record outputs.

Key facts

  • Repeat Module III exercises in CAD.
  • Check alignment before annotation.
  • Record and printed/plotted drawings are assessed.
  • End-semester examination is in CAD practicals only.
Engineering relevance: Digital drawing workflows are used in design offices, manufacturing and documentation.
Orthographic and sectional views in CAD

Develop at least three orthographic projections and at least two sectional views of simple engineering objects in CAD, drawing the same exercises practised in Module III. Include dimensions and text where required.

  1. Open a prepared setup.
  2. Create or import the object geometry.
  3. Generate aligned orthographic views.
  4. Create a full section and apply hatch.
  5. Add dimensions/text, save and plot or print for record.
Malayalam support: Module III-ൽ practice ചെയ്ത exercises Module IV-ൽ CAD-ൽ repeat ചെയ്യുക; output and record both maintain ചെയ്യുക.

Module summary

Accuracy includes geometry, annotation, file management and presentation.

Practice

Module-wise questions and answers

Module 1 questions

Name two polygon constructions in the practical list.
Pentagon and hexagon.
Name the two prescribed ellipse methods.
Rectangular method and concentric-circle method.
What is surface development?
Unfolding a 3D surface into a plane pattern.

Module 2 questions

What are first- and third-angle projections?
Standard projection conventions determining view relationships and placement.
What is the purpose of a reference line?
It provides the datum for transferring projections between planes.
What minimum orthographic/sectional exercises are prescribed?
At least three orthographic and two sectional exercises.

Module 3 questions

Name three draw commands.
Line, circle and arc are examples.
What do osnap and grid support?
Accurate coordinate and point selection during drafting.
Why use modify commands?
To edit geometry efficiently without redrawing it.

Module 4 questions

How many CAD orthographic exercises are required?
Minimum three.
How many CAD sectional exercises are required?
Minimum two.
What is included in the ESE split?
Short CO1/CO2 items, CAD drawing, viva and record.

Last-day revision

Quick revision and exam checklist

Manual drawing checklist

  • Set sheet, margin and title block.
  • Use construction lines lightly.
  • Apply the prescribed construction method.
  • Keep projection views aligned.
  • Submit a neat certified record.

CAD checklist

  • Set units, limits, grid, snap and osnap.
  • Save before and after major modifications.
  • Check line colour, weight and type.
  • Add dimensions and text legibly.
  • Plot/print and attach required record evidence.
