Terms of Use

Rules and limitations for using POLY PMNA study resources and interactive services.

Educational purpose

POLY PMNA is an independent study-support service. It is not SITTTR Kerala, an examination authority, a college administration system or an official result service.

Official-source priority

Official SITTTR pages, government or institutional notices, approved syllabi and examination instructions take priority over every POLY PMNA page. Report any mismatch instead of relying on it.

AI and generated content

Ask POLY AI and automatically generated lesson handbooks can contain mistakes, omissions or outdated interpretations. AI output must not be treated as an official syllabus statement, final engineering design, legal advice, medical advice or guaranteed examination answer. Verify important facts against the cited official source or an instructor.

Quizzes and grading

Daily Quiz scores are personal practice records calculated in the browser. They are not proctored or tamper-proof. Mock-exam grading may use AI and rubric automation. A browser-only fallback score is provisional and must not be treated as a verified published result. Even a server-evaluated score is study feedback, not an official academic mark.

Accounts and security

You are responsible for activity under your account and for protecting your login credentials. Do not attempt to access another user's data, bypass limits, forge results, interfere with deployment, scrape services abusively or exploit the website.

Acceptable content

Do not submit unlawful, threatening, abusive, discriminatory, sexually exploitative, privacy-invasive, malicious or copyrighted content that you do not have permission to share. Public Help comments must remain relevant to the website or student support.

External resources

External websites, AI providers, file hosts and official portals are controlled by their own operators. POLY PMNA does not guarantee their availability, accuracy, security or policies.

Availability and changes

Features may be corrected, restricted, removed or temporarily unavailable. Content and these terms may change when the curriculum, data sources, services or legal requirements change.

Corrections

Use the Help page to report a wrong subject code, broken link, incorrect lesson, privacy issue or grading problem. Provide the page URL and exact error without sharing sensitive personal data.
