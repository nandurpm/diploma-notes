Terms of Use

Basic terms for using the Diploma Notes study-resource website and student-account services.

Purpose

This website provides study-navigation links, educational material and optional daily-exam participation for Polytechnic students.

Accuracy

Materials may be corrected or updated. Students should verify syllabus, examination, curriculum, institution and schedule details with the relevant official source.

Student accounts

Create only one account for yourself, provide a recovery email you control, choose the correct district and college, and keep your password confidential. Do not share accounts, impersonate another student, automate account creation, attempt to discover other users' details, or bypass account and exam restrictions.

Accounts may be suspended when misuse, false information, security abuse or unfair exam participation is detected. A verified recovery email may be required before an account can access daily exams.

Daily exams

Exam availability, timing, duration and result release may vary. Students must submit their own work and follow the instructions shown for each exam. Technical safeguards reduce misuse but cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the discussion area, attempt to disrupt the website, probe protected database collections, overload account endpoints, or submit unlawful or harmful content.

External resources

The website does not control the availability or policies of external websites and file-hosting services.
