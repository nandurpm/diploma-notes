What to include

  • Page URL and curriculum revision
  • Department, semester and subject code
  • Affected button such as View Lesson or Download Notes
  • Expected result and actual result
  • Screenshot when the issue is visual

Choose the correct curriculum first

Revision 2026 is the current curriculum directory. Revision 2021 is retained for legacy batches. Do not report a missing 2026 lesson using a 2021 department page.
