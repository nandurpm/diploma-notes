Module I

Electrical Measurements & Home Installations

Instructional Hours: 9 | CO Mapped: CO1
Expt 1: Testing and Measuring Equipment

This experiment focuses on using the Digital Multimeter (DMM) and Clamp Meter.

  • Multimeter: Measuring AC/DC Voltage, Resistance, and Continuity.
  • Non-Contact Voltage (NCV): Detecting live wires without physical contact.
  • Clamp Meter: Measuring high AC current by clamping around a single conductor (works on the principle of Mutual Induction).
Malayalam support: മൾട്ടിമീറ്റർ ഉപയോഗിക്കുമ്പോൾ വോൾട്ടേജ് അളക്കാൻ പാരലൽ ആയും കറന്റ് അളക്കാൻ സീരീസ് ആയും കണക്ട് ചെയ്യണം. കണ്ടിന്യൂയിറ്റി ടെസ്റ്റ് വയർ മുറിഞ്ഞിട്ടുണ്ടോ എന്ന് പരിശോധിക്കാൻ സഹായിക്കുന്നു.
Expt 2: Extension Box Wiring

Wiring two sockets controlled by separate switches. Key focus: Wire sizing and Earthing.

Safety Check: Always connect the Phase (Line) wire to the switch. The Neutral wire goes directly to the socket. The Earth wire (Green) must be connected to the larger top pin of the socket.
Expt 3 & 4: Fan Connections & Inverters

Ceiling Fan: Wiring an induction motor fan with a regulator. Modern upgrade: Replacing with a BLDC (Brushless DC) fan which is more energy-efficient.

Home Inverter: Connecting a battery (Tall Tubular) to an inverter to provide backup for critical loads like lights and fans.

Module III

Electronics Assembly & Circuits

Instructional Hours: 9 | CO Mapped: CO3
Expt 7: Breadboard & Soldering

Breadboard: Used for temporary circuit prototyping without soldering.

PCB Soldering: Permanent assembly. Practice de-soldering using a pump or wick.

Expt 8 & 9: Rectifiers & Clippers

Rectifiers: Converting AC to DC.

  • Half Wave: Uses 1 diode.
  • Full Wave (Bridge): Uses 4 diodes.

Clippers: Circuits used to "clip" or remove portions of a waveform above or below a certain level.

Arduino Controller & Programming

Introduction to Arduino UNO and the Arduino IDE.

  • LED Blinking: Writing the first 'Hello World' of hardware.
  • Alternating LEDs: Logic for flashing two LEDs.
  • Voice Control: Interfacing Arduino with a voice module or smartphone to control lights.

Formula & Procedure Bank

Ohm's Law (Context: Resistor selection for LED) R = (V_supply - V_led) / I_led

Example: To run a 2V LED from 5V at 20mA: R = (5-2)/0.02 = 150Ω

Standard Wiring Colors (India)

  • Phase: Red / Yellow / Blue
  • Neutral: Black
  • Earth: Green or Green-Yellow

Module-wise Q&A

What is the purpose of a Clamp Meter? (Module 1)
A clamp meter is used to measure current in a live conductor without breaking the circuit. It uses the principle of magnetic induction.
Difference between MCB and RCCB? (Module 2)
MCB protects against overcurrent and short circuits. RCCB protects against earth leakage current and prevents electric shocks to humans.
Why is a resistor used with an LED? (Module 3)
LEDs have low internal resistance. A series resistor is used to limit the current flowing through the LED to prevent it from burning out.
What are the two main functions in an Arduino sketch? (Module 4)
1. setup(): Runs once to initialize settings. 2. loop(): Runs repeatedly to control the logic.

Quick Revision & Checklist

Key Points

  • Always check continuity before powering a circuit.
  • Phase wire must always go through the switch.
  • Use 1N4007 diodes for general rectification.
  • Arduino pins can provide max 40mA (keep it safe).
