Official module 1

Automotive battery and electronics foundations

The official contents cover conductors, insulators, current, potential difference, power, EMF, resistance, Ohm’s law, series and parallel circuits, electromagnetism, AC/DC generation, capacitance, inductance, semiconductors, diodes, thermistors, SCRs, transistors, gates, ICs, battery types, construction, charging, jump starting and troubles.

Hours: 15
CO1 · Understanding · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Use basic electrical terms in an automotive context.
  • Explain battery construction and charging.
  • Identify common battery faults.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M1.01 — Basic electrical terms and circuits (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

Voltage is potential difference, current is charge flow, resistance opposes current and power is the rate of electrical energy transfer. Ohm’s law and series/parallel rules provide the first diagnostic model.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • State units and symbols.
  • Apply Ohm’s law.
  • Distinguish series and parallel paths.
Engineering / workplace application: A technician uses voltage-drop and continuity checks to locate wiring faults.
M1.02 — Automotive electronic components (5 hours)

Concept and explanation

Semiconductors form the basis of diodes, thermistors, SCRs and transistors. Their construction and controlled conduction allow rectification, sensing, switching and regulation.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare P-type and N-type material.
  • State diode and transistor uses.
  • Recognise a sensor or switching role.
M1.03 — Battery types, construction and ratings (5 hours)

Concept and explanation

Lead-acid, nickel-cadmium and lithium-ion batteries differ in chemistry, construction, voltage behaviour and maintenance. Capacity, cold-cranking ability, specific gravity and charging requirements are important ratings.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare battery chemistries.
  • Explain specific gravity for lead-acid cells.
  • Read basic battery ratings.
Common mistake: Use correct polarity, ventilation and protective equipment when handling batteries.
M1.04 — Battery charging, jump starting and troubles (2 hours)

Concept and explanation

Charging restores stored energy under controlled voltage and current. Jump starting requires correct polarity and a safe connection sequence; troubles include low electrolyte, sulfation, poor terminals and internal failure.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • List safe jump-start steps.
  • Relate symptoms to likely causes.
  • Inspect terminals and connections.
Module summary: Battery condition affects starting, charging, lighting and electronic control reliability.

Charging and starting systems

Automotive charging content includes AC charging operation, alternator construction, rotor, stator, rectifier, output control, regulators, cranking circuits, starting-motor operation, motor types, gear reduction and Bendix, axial and solenoid drives.

Hours: 14
CO2 · Understanding · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Explain starter-motor engagement.
  • Identify output-control methods.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M2.01 — Automotive alternator construction and operation (4 hours)

Concept and explanation

An alternator uses electromagnetic induction to generate AC, which is rectified to DC for the vehicle electrical system. Rotor, stator, rectifier and regulator each have a distinct function.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Identify alternator components.
  • Explain AC generation and rectification.
  • Relate rotor speed to output.
M2.02 — Alternator output control and regulators (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

Output control prevents overcharging and stabilises system voltage as engine speed and electrical load vary. Regulators control field current or use electronic switching.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • State why regulation is necessary.
  • Explain field-current control.
  • Identify symptoms of overcharging and undercharging.
M2.03 — Starting system and cranking circuit (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

The cranking circuit includes battery, switch, relay/solenoid, starter motor and cables. The motor converts electrical energy into torque sufficient to rotate the engine during starting.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Trace the cranking path.
  • Explain voltage drop in heavy cables.
  • Identify starter components.
M2.04 — Starting drives and gear reduction (4 hours)

Concept and explanation

Bendix, axial and solenoid-operated drives engage the pinion with the flywheel and disengage after the engine starts. Gear reduction increases starting torque while allowing a compact motor.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare drive mechanisms.
  • Explain engagement and release.
  • Relate gear ratio to torque.
Common mistake: Keep hands clear of moving parts and isolate the battery before starter removal.
Module summary: Charging and starting systems must deliver high current briefly while protecting the battery and electrical loads.

M3.01 — Purpose and functions of ignition systems (2 hours)

Concept and explanation

An ignition system stores electrical energy and releases it as a high-voltage spark at the correct crank angle. It must provide adequate energy across the plug gap under varying speed and load.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • State ignition requirements.
  • Relate spark energy to combustion.
  • Identify timing as a control variable.
M3.02 — Types of ignition systems (5 hours)

Concept and explanation

Contact-breaker and distributor systems use mechanical switching, while electronic, distributorless, waste-spark and coil-on-plug systems use electronic control and individual or paired coils.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare mechanical and electronic systems.
  • Explain waste-spark and coil-on-plug layouts.
  • Identify a suitable application.
M3.03 — Ignition components and triggering (5 hours)

Concept and explanation

The ignition coil, control module, powertrain control module, magnetic sensor, Hall-effect switch, optical sensor and spark plug form the signal-to-spark chain.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Describe component functions.
  • Trace trigger signal flow.
  • Explain spark-plug construction.
M3.04 — Ignition timing and advance (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

Ignition timing is the crank position at which the spark occurs. Advance mechanisms adjust timing with speed and load so combustion pressure develops at a useful point after top dead centre.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Define advance and retard.
  • Explain why fixed timing is insufficient.
  • Identify symptoms of incorrect timing.
Module summary: Correct ignition timing and a healthy spark are essential for efficient combustion and emission control.

M4.01 — Automobile wiring and protection (5 hours)

Concept and explanation

Automotive wiring uses conductors, ground paths, battery cables, terminals, connectors, fuses, circuit breakers and relays. Schematics show how power and control signals move through a circuit.
