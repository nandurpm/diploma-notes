Objectives, Course Outcomes and CO–PO Information

Official course objectives

  1. To understand the different fuel systems and components in Petrol and Diesel engines.
  2. To impart basic knowledge on the necessity of lubrication and cooling systems in automobiles.
  3. To familiarize with intake and exhaust systems.

Course Outcomes

  1. Identify fuel systems employed in petrol vehicles.
  2. Illustrate the working of fuel system employed in diesel vehicles.
  3. Explain the working of Engine lubrication and cooling systems.
  4. Outline the details of components in Intake and Exhaust systems used in engines.

Official module 1

Petrol fuel systems

The official content covers fuel-feed pumps, filters, air cleaners, carburettors, air–fuel ratios and stoichiometric mixture; EFI types, fuel-system components, sensors, actuators, idle control and onboard diagnostics.

Hours: 15
CO1 · Applying · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Trace fuel from tank to cylinder.
  • Compare carburettor and EFI systems.
  • Identify sensors, actuators and diagnostic functions.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M1.01 — Conventional petrol fuel system (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

A conventional petrol system uses tank, feed pump, filter, air cleaner and carburettor to prepare a combustible mixture. The air–fuel ratio must vary between starting, idling, acceleration and cruising.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Name feed-system components.
  • Explain the role of the air cleaner.
  • Define stoichiometric air–fuel ratio.
M1.02 — Carburettor components and circuits (5 hours)

Concept and explanation

A simple carburettor meters fuel using pressure difference. Circuits for float control, idle, progression, main operation, acceleration and enrichment support different engine conditions.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Explain venturi action.
  • Identify major circuits.
  • Relate mixture symptoms to faults.
M1.03 — Electronic fuel injection systems (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

EFI meters fuel electronically using sensors, an electronic control unit and injectors. Single-point, multi-point and direct injection differ in injector location and control strategy.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare injection types.
  • Identify basic sensors and actuators.
  • Explain closed-loop control at a high level.
M1.04 — Gasoline direct injection and diagnostics (4 hours)

Concept and explanation

Gasoline direct injection places the injector in the cylinder and requires accurate high-pressure delivery and control. On-board diagnostics monitor signals and store fault information.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • State the advantage of direct injection.
  • Explain pressure and timing needs.
  • Use diagnostic evidence rather than guessing.
Module summary: Fuel preparation controls power, economy, starting, emissions and drivability.

Diesel fuel systems

Diesel content includes conventional layouts, primary and secondary filters, jerk and distributor pumps, inline and distributor FIP, feed pumps, injectors, pneumatic and centrifugal governors, electronic diesel control and common-rail components.

Hours: 14
CO2 · Understanding · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Trace a diesel fuel path.
  • Compare injection-pump types.
  • Explain common-rail pressure and control.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M2.01 — Conventional diesel fuel system (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

A conventional diesel path includes tank, feed pump, filters, injection pump, high-pressure lines and injectors. Filtration and bleeding are important because air or contamination affects injection.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Draw the layout.
  • State filter purposes.
  • Explain the need for priming.
M2.02 — Diesel components and fuel filters (5 hours)

Concept and explanation

Primary and secondary filters remove contaminants at different stages. Fuel injectors atomise fuel and governors regulate delivery as speed changes.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare filter stages.
  • Explain injector function.
  • Distinguish pneumatic and centrifugal governors.
M2.03 — Fuel-injection pumps (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

Jerk and distributor pumps create and distribute high-pressure fuel. Inline pumps use separate pumping elements, whereas distributor pumps use one pumping element with distribution.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare inline and distributor pumps.
  • Explain feed-pump support.
  • Relate timing to injection.
M2.04 — Common-rail diesel system (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

A common-rail system separates pressure generation from injection timing. Tank, delivery pump, high-pressure pump, rail, pressure sensor, electronic injectors, relief valve, glow plugs and emission controls operate as a coordinated system.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Identify common-rail components.
  • Explain electronic injection control.
  • State the role of glow plugs and emission devices.
Module summary: Diesel systems require clean fuel, accurate injection timing and high pressure to form a suitable spray.
Official module 3

Engine lubrication and cooling

Lubrication content includes oil properties, grades, additives, petroil, splash, pressure, dry-sump and wet-sump systems, oil pumps, filters, coolers and relief valves. Cooling includes air and water systems, pumps, radiators, thermostats, expansion tanks, coolants, antifreeze and pressure caps.

Hours: 15
CO3 · Understanding · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Explain why lubrication and cooling are necessary.
  • Compare system layouts and components.
  • Relate symptoms to overheating or oil faults.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M3.01 — Lubricating oil properties and systems (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

Viscosity, viscosity index, flash point and additives influence oil performance. Petroil, splash, pressure, wet-sump and dry-sump systems deliver oil to moving parts in different ways.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare single- and multi-grade oil.
  • Explain additives.
  • Trace a pressure-lubrication path.
M3.02 — Oil pumps, filters and coolers (4 hours)

Concept and explanation

Gear, vane and rotor pumps create flow; filters remove particles; coolers control oil temperature; relief valves protect the circuit from excessive pressure. Full-flow and bypass filtration have different arrangements.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare pump types.
  • Explain full-flow and bypass filtering.
  • State the function of a relief valve.
M3.03 — Air and water cooling (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

Air cooling transfers heat through fins, whereas water cooling circulates coolant through jackets, pump, radiator and hoses. The system must reject heat without excessive temperature variation.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare air and water cooling.
  • Identify the main components.
  • Explain coolant circulation.
M3.04 — Thermostat, radiator and antifreeze (5 hours)

Concept and explanation

A thermostat controls warm-up and operating temperature. Radiators, expansion tanks, pressure caps and antifreeze solutions manage heat transfer, volume change, boiling protection and corrosion.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Explain thermostat operation.
  • State why pressure caps are used.
  • List safe cooling-system service steps.
Common mistake: Never open a hot pressurised cooling system; allow it to cool before inspection.
Module summary: Lubrication controls friction and wear, while cooling keeps engine temperature within a safe operating range.

Intake, exhaust and turbocharging

The module covers intake manifolds, air cleaners, variable intake, electronic throttle, VVT/CVVT/VTVT/VVT-i, exhaust manifold, mufflers, resonator, catalytic converter, tail pipe, muffler types and normal, waste-gate and variable-geometry turbochargers.

CO4 · Understanding · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Compare valve-timing and muffler technologies.

M4.01 — Valve timing and intake technologies (4 hours)

Concept and explanation

Intake manifolds and air cleaners guide and condition air. Variable valve timing and variable intake systems alter breathing with speed and load; electronic throttle controls air demand.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Explain valve timing terms.
  • Compare fixed and variable systems.
  • Relate intake restriction to performance.
M4.02 — Mufflers and exhaust treatment (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

Exhaust manifolds collect gases; resonators and mufflers reduce noise; catalytic converters assist emission control. Absorber, baffle, wave-cancellation and resonance types use different acoustic principles.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Identify exhaust components.
  • Compare muffler constructions.
  • State the purpose of a catalytic converter.
M4.03 — Turbocharging system (4 hours)

Concept and explanation

A turbocharger uses exhaust energy to drive a turbine connected to a compressor. Compressed intake air increases charge density, but heat, lubrication, control and response must be managed.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Trace exhaust-to-intake energy flow.
  • Explain boost pressure.
  • State the need for intercooling or control.
M4.04 — Turbocharger types and turbo lag (3 hours)

Concept and explanation

Normal, waste-gate and variable-geometry turbochargers control boost differently. Turbo lag is the delay before useful boost develops after a change in engine demand.

Malayalam support: ഈ ആശയം മനസ്സിലാക്കുമ്പോൾ English technical terms മാറ്റാതെ പഠിക്കുക.

Study points

  • Compare waste-gate and variable geometry.
  • Explain turbo lag.
  • List service precautions.
Module summary: Gas exchange determines engine breathing, while exhaust treatment controls noise and emissions.
