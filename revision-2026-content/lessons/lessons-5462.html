Objectives, outcomes and mapping

Course objectives

  • ● To understand the different types of hazards in the oil and gas industry, including
  • chemical, toxic, explosion, and mechanical hazards.
  • ● To learn about the safety aspects related to petroleum hydrocarbons, sour gases, and
  • their effects on human health.
  • ● To explore safety management practices, environmental considerations, and offshore
  • safety protocols.
  • ● To familiarize students with various risk analysis techniques and emergency
  • management plans for both onshore and offshore operations.
