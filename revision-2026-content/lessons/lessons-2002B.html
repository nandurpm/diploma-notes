Mechanics

1. Units, Fundamental and Derived Quantities

A physical quantity is a measurable property expressed by a numerical value and unit. Fundamental quantities are independent; derived quantities are formed from them.

Fundamental quantitySI unitSymbol

Timeseconds

Examples of derived quantities: velocity (m/s), acceleration (m/s²), force (N = kg·m/s²), work (J = N·m), power (W = J/s).

Malayalam: Physical Quantity എന്നത് അളക്കാൻ കഴിയുന്ന ഒരു property ആണ്. Fundamental Quantities മറ്റൊരു quantity ഉപയോഗിച്ച് നിർവചിക്കാത്ത അടിസ്ഥാന quantities ആണ്.
2. Scalars, Vectors and Resolution

A scalar has magnitude only; a vector has magnitude and direction. Vector addition follows Triangle Law and Parallelogram Law. A vector A at angle θ has rectangular components Aₓ = A cosθ and Aᵧ = A sinθ.

  • Scalars: mass, time, temperature, speed, energy.
  • Vectors: displacement, velocity, acceleration, force, momentum.
  • Triangle Law: place the tail of the second vector at the head of the first.
  • Parallelogram Law: diagonal through the common origin gives the resultant.
Malayalam: Vector-ന് magnitude-ഉം direction-ഉം ഉണ്ടായിരിക്കും. Resolution of Vector എന്നത് Vector-നെ horizontal, vertical components ആയി വേർതിരിക്കുന്നതാണ്.

Vector component animation

3. Displacement, Velocity, Acceleration and Equations of Motion

Distance is total path length; displacement is the shortest directed change in position. Speed is distance/time, velocity is displacement/time, and acceleration is rate of change of velocity.

v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as

Solved example

A vehicle starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 s.

Given: u=0, a=2, t=5

v: 0+(2×5)=10 m/s

s: 0+(½×2×25)=25 m

4. Newton’s Laws, Momentum and Recoil
  • First Law: a body remains at rest or uniform straight-line motion unless acted upon by an unbalanced external force.
  • Second Law: force equals rate of change of momentum; for constant mass, F=ma.
  • Third Law: every action has an equal and opposite reaction acting on another body.
  • Momentum: p=mv, unit kg·m/s.

For an isolated system, total momentum before interaction equals total momentum after interaction. In gun recoil, forward bullet momentum equals backward gun momentum in magnitude.

Malayalam: Inertia എന്നത് ഒരു body അതിന്റെ നിലവിലെ state മാറ്റത്തെ എതിർക്കുന്ന property ആണ്. Recoil-ൽ bullet-ന്റെ forward momentum-ന് തുല്യമായ opposite momentum gun-ന് ലഭിക്കുന്നു.

Recoil animation

5. Work, Energy, Conservation of Energy and Power

Work is done when a force causes displacement: W=Fs cosθ. Work is positive when force assists displacement, negative when it opposes it, and zero when force is perpendicular to displacement or displacement is zero.

Kinetic Energy is energy due to motion, KE=½mv². Gravitational Potential Energy is PE=mgh. In ideal free fall, PE decreases while KE increases, but total mechanical energy remains constant. Power is rate of doing work, P=W/t, measured in watt.

Malayalam: Freely falling body താഴേക്ക് വരുമ്പോൾ Potential Energy കുറയുകയും Kinetic Energy കൂടുകയും ചെയ്യും. Ideal condition-ൽ total Mechanical Energy constant ആണ്.

Free-fall energy transformation

Module II

Properties of Matter and Thermodynamics

10 Theory HoursCO24 Listed Experiments
Malayalam overview: Elasticity, Stress, Strain, Fluid Flow, Bernoulli’s Theorem, Viscosity, Heat Transfer എന്നിവ material-ുകളുടെയും fluid-ുകളുടെയും practical behaviour മനസ്സിലാക്കാൻ സഹായിക്കുന്നു.
1. Elasticity, Stress, Strain and Hooke’s Law

Elasticity is the property by which a body regains original dimensions after removal of deforming force, within its elastic limit. Stress is internal restoring force per unit area; strain is fractional change in dimension.

Stress = Force/Area
Strain = Change in dimension/Original dimension
Young’s Modulus = Longitudinal Stress/Longitudinal Strain
Rigidity Modulus = Shearing Stress/Shearing Strain
Bulk Modulus = Volume Stress/Volume Strain

Hooke’s Law: within the elastic limit, stress is directly proportional to strain. For a spring, F=kx.

Malayalam: Stress എന്നത് unit area-ൽ പ്രവർത്തിക്കുന്ന internal restoring force ആണ്. Strain എന്നത് dimension-ൽ വന്ന change-ന്റെ original dimension-നോടുള്ള ratio ആണ്.

Solved example

A 2 m wire of area 1 mm² extends by 1 mm under 100 N.

Y=(F/A)/(ΔL/L)=(100/1×10⁻⁶)/(1×10⁻³/2)=2×10¹¹ Pa.

2. Streamline Flow, Turbulent Flow and Continuity

In streamline flow, every fluid particle follows a smooth path and velocity at a point is steady. Turbulent flow has irregular eddies and rapidly changing velocity. For incompressible steady flow, mass conservation gives A₁v₁=A₂v₂.

Malayalam: Pipe-ന്റെ cross-sectional area കുറയുമ്പോൾ Fluid Velocity കൂടുന്നു. ഇത് Equation of Continuity കാണിക്കുന്നു.

Streamline / Turbulent comparison

3. Bernoulli’s Theorem and Atomizer

For steady flow of an ideal fluid along a streamline, pressure energy, kinetic energy and potential energy per unit volume remain constant.

P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant

In an atomizer, fast air across the top of a tube produces lower pressure. Higher pressure on the liquid surface pushes liquid up the tube and breaks it into droplets.

Malayalam: Fluid Velocity കൂടുന്ന ഭാഗത്ത് pressure കുറയാം. ഈ pressure difference ഉപയോഗിച്ചാണ് Atomizer liquid മുകളിലേക്ക് ഉയർത്തി spray ചെയ്യുന്നത്.

Atomizer principle

4. Viscosity, Viscous Force and Stoke’s Law

Viscosity is internal resistance to relative motion between fluid layers. The coefficient of viscosity η measures this resistance. For a small sphere moving slowly through a viscous liquid:

F = 6πηrv

At terminal velocity, net force becomes zero and the sphere moves with constant speed. Viscosity of liquids generally decreases as temperature increases.

Malayalam: Viscosity ഒരു fluid-ന്റെ flow-നെ എതിർക്കുന്ന internal resistance ആണ്. Honey-ക്ക് water-നെക്കാൾ viscosity കൂടുതലാണ്.
5. Heat, Temperature, Scales and Heat Transfer

Heat is energy transferred due to temperature difference; temperature indicates degree of hotness and determines heat-flow direction. Common scales are Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

F=(9/5)C+32
C=(5/9)(F−32)
K=C+273.15

Enter °C and convert.

Modes of heat transfer

  • Conduction: transfer through particle interaction, mainly in solids.
  • Convection: transfer by bulk motion of a fluid.
  • Radiation: transfer by electromagnetic waves; no medium required.
Malayalam: Conduction-ൽ bulk movement ഇല്ല. Convection-ൽ fluid movement ഉണ്ട്. Radiation-ന് medium ആവശ്യമില്ല.
Module III

Rotational and Wave Motion

11 Theory HoursCO35 Listed Experiments
Malayalam overview: Circular Motion, Moment of Inertia, Torque, Angular Momentum, SHM, Wave Motion എന്നിവ rotating machines, vibration, sound എന്നീ applications-നോട് ബന്ധപ്പെടുത്തി പഠിക്കുന്നു.
1. Circular Motion and Angular Quantities

Circular motion is motion along a circular path. Angular displacement θ is measured in radian; angular velocity ω=dθ/dt; angular acceleration α=dω/dt.

v = rω
aₜ = rα
aᶜ = v²/r = rω²
Fᶜ = mv²/r = mrω²

Velocity is tangential, while centripetal acceleration and force point toward the centre.

Malayalam: Circular Motion-ൽ velocity direction തുടർച്ചയായി മാറുന്നു. Centre-ലേക്ക് directed ആയ acceleration ആണ് Centripetal Acceleration.

Circular motion vectors

2. Rigid Body, Moment of Inertia and Radius of Gyration

A rigid body is an ideal body whose particle separations remain constant. Moment of Inertia measures rotational inertia and depends on mass distribution and chosen axis.

I = Σmr²
I = Mk²

3. Axis Theorems, Torque and Angular Momentum

Parallel Axis Theorem: I=Icm+Md². Perpendicular Axis Theorem: for a plane lamina, Iz=Ix+Iy. Torque is turning effect, τ=rF sinθ. Angular momentum is L=Iω and remains constant when external torque is zero.

Conservation of angular momentum

4. Periodic Motion and Simple Harmonic Motion

Periodic motion repeats after equal time intervals. In SHM, restoring force is proportional to displacement and directed toward mean position. Important quantities are amplitude, time period and frequency.

For simple pendulum: T = 2π√(l/g)
For spring-mass system: T = 2π√(m/k)
Malayalam: SHM-ൽ Restoring Force displacement-ന് proportional ആയിരിക്കുകയും Mean Position-ലേക്ക് directed ആയിരിക്കുകയും ചെയ്യും.

Simple harmonic motion

5. Transverse and Longitudinal Waves

A wave transfers energy without net transport of matter. In transverse waves, particles vibrate perpendicular to propagation; in longitudinal waves, particles vibrate parallel, forming compressions and rarefactions.

v = fλ
T = 1/f

Wave characteristics: velocity, time period, frequency, wavelength, amplitude and phase.

Malayalam: Transverse Wave-ൽ particles wave direction-ന് perpendicular ആയി vibrate ചെയ്യും. Longitudinal Wave-ൽ particles wave direction-ന് parallel ആയി vibrate ചെയ്യും.

Module IV

Modern Physics

10 Theory HoursCO42 Listed Experiments
Malayalam overview: Semiconductor, Doping, PN Junction, Photoelectric Effect, Photon, Laser എന്നീ concepts electronic devices, sensors, computing hardware എന്നിവയുടെ അടിസ്ഥാനമാണ്.
1. Energy Bands and Classification of Solids

In solids, closely spaced atomic energy levels form bands. The highest occupied band is the Valence Band; the next allowed band is the Conduction Band. The forbidden gap determines electrical behaviour.

  • Conductor: bands overlap or conduction band is partly filled.
  • Semiconductor: small forbidden gap.
  • Insulator: large forbidden gap.
2. Intrinsic, Extrinsic, N-type and P-type Semiconductors

An intrinsic semiconductor is pure silicon or germanium. Thermal energy creates electron–hole pairs. Doping adds controlled impurity atoms. Pentavalent donor impurity forms N-type material with electrons as majority carriers; trivalent acceptor impurity forms P-type material with holes as majority carriers.

Malayalam: Pure Semiconductor-ലേക്ക് ചെറിയ അളവിൽ impurity ചേർത്ത് conductivity മാറ്റുന്ന process ആണ് Doping. N-type-ൽ electrons majority carriers ആണ്; P-type-ൽ holes majority carriers ആണ്.
3. PN Junction, Forward Bias, Reverse Bias and V–I Characteristics

When P-type and N-type materials join, carrier diffusion and recombination produce a depletion region and barrier potential. In forward bias, P is connected to positive and N to negative, reducing the barrier. In reverse bias, the barrier increases and only a small reverse saturation current flows.

The diode V–I curve contains forward knee voltage, rapid forward current rise and small reverse current. Typical knee voltage is about 0.7 V for silicon and 0.3 V for germanium.

Malayalam: Forward Bias barrier കുറച്ച് current flow വർധിപ്പിക്കുന്നു. Reverse Bias barrier കൂട്ടുന്നതിനാൽ വളരെ ചെറിയ reverse current മാത്രം ഉണ്ടാകും.

Carrier movement in PN junction

4. Quantum Theory, Photon and Photoelectric Effect

Light energy is exchanged in packets called photons. Photon energy is E=hf=hc/λ. The Photoelectric Effect is emission of electrons from a suitable surface when incident light frequency exceeds threshold frequency. Intensity controls number of photoelectrons, while frequency controls maximum kinetic energy.

hf = φ + KEmax
Malayalam: Suitable metal surface-ൽ Threshold Frequency-നേക്കാൾ കൂടുതലുള്ള frequency-യിലുള്ള light പതിക്കുമ്പോൾ electrons പുറപ്പെടുന്ന phenomenon ആണ് Photoelectric Effect.

Photoelectron emission

5. Laser Principle, Characteristics, Applications and Ruby Laser

LASER means Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation. Laser action requires pumping, metastable state, population inversion, stimulated emission and an optical resonator.

  • Stimulated absorption: atom absorbs photon and rises to higher level.
  • Spontaneous emission: excited atom emits randomly.
  • Stimulated emission: incident photon produces a second identical photon.
  • Population inversion: more atoms in upper laser level than lower level.

Laser light is monochromatic, coherent, directional, intense and low-divergence. Applications include communication, medicine, cutting, welding, measurement, alignment, barcode reading and data storage.

Ruby Laser: synthetic ruby rod doped with chromium ions is pumped by a flash lamp. One end mirror is fully reflecting and the other partially reflecting. It is a three-level solid-state laser.

Malayalam: Higher energy state-ൽ lower energy state-നേക്കാൾ കൂടുതൽ atoms ഉണ്ടാകുന്ന condition ആണ് Population Inversion. Laser action-ന് ഇത് ആവശ്യമാണ്.

Weeks 1–4 — Module I

  1. Classify fundamental and derived quantities; practise Vector Addition and Resolution.
  2. Solve Equations of Motion; demonstrate Newton’s First Law; collect daily-life Inertia examples.
  3. Find Newton’s II and III Law examples; list appliance power ratings and calculate one-hour energy use.
  4. Calculate Work in climbing stairs or lifting water; relate food energy to daily activity.

Weeks 5–8 — Module II

  1. Hooke’s Law with rubber band and coins; Elasticity applications; Young’s Modulus practice; concept map.
  2. Identify Streamline/Turbulent Flow; qualitative continuity; paper-strip Bernoulli activity; applications.
  3. Compare viscosity of water, oil and honey; observe drop time; study temperature effect.
  4. Ice–water phase-change observation; compare heating of materials; temperature conversions; Heat Transfer examples.

Weeks 9–12 — Official enrichment

Official mismatch note: These activities appear in the official table but are outside the listed core Module III theory.
  1. Mirror and light observations.
  2. Convex lens, optical instruments, Total Internal Reflection and Optical Fibre.
  3. Static Electricity, magnets, appliance current/voltage, Kirchhoff’s Laws.
  4. Conductor resistivity, Electric Generator and magnetic field due to current.

Weeks 13–16 — Module IV

  1. Semiconductor applications, Band Theory, Fermi level enrichment and device study.
  2. PN Junction simulation, diode applications, temperature effect, dynamic resistance and saturation current.
  3. Photoelectric demonstration, flow chart, Einstein-equation numericals and quiz.
  4. Spontaneous versus Stimulated Emission, laser applications and Ruby Laser energy-level diagram.
