Objectives, Course Outcomes and CO–PO Information

Official course objectives

  1. To enable the students to acquire 120 wpm speed in shorthand writing through practice on different topics.
  2. To familiarize with business, political, banking, stockbroking, technical, legal, insurance, shipping and theological phrases.
  3. To apply special contractions with ease and accuracy.

Course Outcomes

  1. Apply speed in shorthand writing at 100 wpm, practise essential vowels, special contraction and advanced phraseography.
  2. Develop dictation speed at 110 wpm and apply intersections, business, political, banking and stockbroking phrases.
  3. Develop dictation speed at 120 wpm and apply insurance, shipping, technical, railway and legal phrases.
  4. Practise dictation at 120 wpm for 7 minutes, transcription, theological phrases and a special list of words.
