Course introduction

Energy Conservation and Audit studies the systematic approach to optimizing energy use in industrial, commercial and residential sectors. It develops the ability to analyze energy consumption patterns, conduct preliminary and detailed energy audits, evaluate energy conservation measures in electrical and thermal systems, and perform economic analysis of energy saving projects while respecting the technical and safety boundaries of electrical and industrial engineering.

Important point: Thermal systems operate at high temperatures and pressures. Do not perform inspections or modifications without authorised pressure-vessel safety clearance and thermal protection.

Explain HVAC and Waste Heat Recovery and state one correct method, application or precaution.

HVAC systems are major energy consumers in buildings. Efficiency can be improved through better insulation, high-efficiency chillers, and heat recovery from exhaust air. Waste heat recovery uses heat from industrial processes for other heating needs.

Answer framework: Describe the concept of 'Waste Heat Recovery' with an example; explain how 'Building Automation Systems' (BAS) contribute to energy saving.

Important point: Refrigerants used in HVAC systems are regulated. Do not handle or charge refrigerants without authorised certification and environmental safety compliance.

Module 4 — Energy Economics and Financial Management

Explain Financial Analysis Tools and state one correct method, application or precaution.

Economic analysis determines the feasibility of energy saving measures. Simple Payback Period (SPP) is the time taken to recover the initial investment. ROI measures the annual return. NPV and LCC provide a more comprehensive view by considering the time value of money.

Answer framework: Calculate the 'Simple Payback Period' for a project with an investment of ₹1,00,000 and annual savings of ₹25,000; define 'Life Cycle Costing'.

Important point: Financial projections are based on assumptions about future energy prices and interest rates. Do not make investment decisions without authorised financial risk assessment and professional audit.

Explain Energy Management Information Systems and state one correct method, application or precaution.

Energy monitoring and targeting involve the systematic collection and analysis of energy data to identify deviations from targets. ESCOs provide energy saving solutions, often through performance-based contracts where they are paid from the savings generated.

Answer framework: Explain the role of an 'Energy Service Company' (ESCO); describe the 'Monitoring and Targeting' (M&T) loop in energy management.

Important point: Performance-based contracts involve complex legal and financial terms. Do not enter into ESCO agreements without authorised legal and technical review of the performance guarantees.
