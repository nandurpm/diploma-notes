Official course objectives

  1. To understand the air-conditioning systems used in automotive applications.
  2. To know the operation of air-conditioning system, thermodynamic aspects involved and servicing of the system.

Course Outcomes

  2. Explain refrigeration and air-conditioning systems.
  3. Outline air-distribution system and its control.
  4. Make use of troubleshooting procedure of air-conditioning system.
