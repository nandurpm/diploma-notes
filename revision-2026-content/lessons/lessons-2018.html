Objectives

Course Objectives and Outcomes

Objectives

  1. Provide hands-on training in basic construction activities.
  2. Enable students to identify and use natural, artificial, and processed construction materials effectively.
  3. Develop practical skills and workmanship by applying theoretical knowledge to real construction practices.

Module 1

Module 1: Bricks, Mortar and Setting Out

Outcomes

Master brick testing, cement mortar preparation, and building layout techniques.

Experiments

  • 1. Determination of dimension, weight and density of bricks.
  • 2. Field test on bricks (dropping, striking, scratching).
  • 3. Cement Mortar preparation (1:3 or 1:6).
  • 4. Setting out of a small building.

Module 2

Module 2: Soils and Flooring Tiles

Outcomes

Identify soil layers in foundation pits and categorize various flooring tiles.

Experiments

  • 5. Report on types of soil in foundation pit.
  • 6. Identification of flooring tiles (Vitrified, Ceramic, Glazed, Mosaic).
Tip: Vitrified tiles are popular for low water absorption and high durability.

Module 3

Module 3: Special Materials and Sand Quality

Outcomes

Analyze bulking of sand and identify special processed construction materials.

Experiments

  • 7. Special processed construction materials report.
  • 8. Bulking of Sand test.
  • 9. Silt Content in Sand determination.
Malayalam Support Note: മണലിലെ ഈർപ്പം മൂലമുള്ള വ്യാപ്തി വർദ്ധനവും (Bulking) മണലിലെ ചെളിയുടെ അളവും കണ്ടെത്തുന്നു.
Module 4

Module 4: Aggregates and Bar Bending

Outcomes

Determine bulk density of aggregates and master bar bending for reinforcement.

Experiments

  • 10. Bulk Density of Fine Aggregate.
  • 11. Bulk Density of Coarse Aggregate.
  • 12. Bar bending (Hooks, Stirrups, Ties, Crank bars).

Model Paper

Practice Model Question Paper

Time: 3 Hours | Max Marks: 75

Part A (2 marks each)

  1. Define Bulking of Sand.
  2. List four types of flooring tiles.
  3. What is the standard size of a brick?
  4. Explain the purpose of setting out a building.
  5. State the use of stirrups in bar bending.

Part B (5 marks each)

  1. Explain the procedure to determine the silt content in sand.
  2. Describe the field tests performed on bricks to check quality.
  3. Discuss the identification features of different flooring tiles.
  4. Explain the center line method for setting out a small building.
Answers

Full Answer Key

Part A: 1. Bulking of sand is the increase in volume due to moisture. 2. Vitrified, Ceramic, Mosaic, Glazed. 3. 19cm x 9cm x 9cm. 4. To transfer the building plan onto the ground. 5. To hold longitudinal bars and resist shear.
