Objectives, Course Outcomes and CO–PO Information

Official course objectives

  1. To introduce vehicle structural crashworthiness and crash testing.
  2. To introduce pedestrian safety.
  3. To get knowledge of sensors provided in the vehicle to avoid a crash and detect obstacles around the vehicle.
  4. To understand fundamentals of sensor data fusion as it relates to ADAS.
  5. To understand the connected vehicle and its role in ADAS and automated vehicles.

Course Outcomes

  1. Explain the basic concept of automobile safety.
  2. Illustrate various passive safety systems used in automobiles.
  3. Illustrate various active safety systems used in automobiles.
  4. Explain vehicle navigation system and various SAE levels of driving automation.
