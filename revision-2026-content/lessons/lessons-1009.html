Objectives, Course Outcomes and CO–PO

Official Course Objectives

  1. Develop awareness about physical fitness and healthy lifestyle practices.
  2. Train students in basic physical exercises, yoga and sports activities.
  3. Create a safe, progressive, methodical and efficient activity-based plan to enhance improvement and minimise risk of injury.
  4. Promote physical activity as a lifetime pursuit and a means to better health.

Module-wise Questions and Answers

What is warm-up?

Warm-up is gradual preparation before more demanding activity. It supports movement readiness and safer transition.

What is an asana?

An asana is a controlled yoga posture performed with alignment and comfortable breathing.

State two stretching precautions.

Warm up, avoid forceful bouncing, breathe normally, move through a comfortable range and stop for sharp pain.

Why are rules important in sports?

Rules support fairness, safety, role clarity and respectful participation.

How can sports skills help engineering teamwork?

Coordination, communication, punctuality, stress control and problem solving transfer to technical team tasks.
