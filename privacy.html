Privacy

How the study website, student accounts and Help discussion handle information.

Reading the study website

The main Diploma Notes study pages are publicly readable. You do not need an account to read lessons, open syllabus links, view notes links, or use the subject browsers.

Student accounts and daily exams

When a student creates an account, the service may store the chosen username, recovery email, selected district, selected college, Firebase user ID, account status, verification status and account timestamps. The recovery email is used for verification and password recovery. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored in the website's Firestore profile documents.

Username availability, account creation, username-based login mapping and password-reset requests are processed by protected Firebase Cloud Functions. Basic rate-limit records may be temporarily stored to reduce automated abuse. Daily-exam participation may also store exam starts, submitted answers, scores and submission timestamps when those features are enabled.

Help discussion

The Help page uses Firebase anonymous authentication and Cloud Firestore for public comments and replies. When you post, the public display name you enter, your comment or reply text, an anonymous Firebase user ID, and timestamps may be stored. The browser may also save your chosen display name in localStorage so you do not need to type it again.

Top-level comments are soft-deleted when the author deletes them, so replies can remain understandable. Replies posted by the same anonymous user can be deleted by that user. Deleted comments may still leave a public placeholder such as “This comment was deleted.”

Do not share private details

Do not post passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, private IDs, account details, or sensitive personal information in the public discussion. Never share your student-account password with another person.

External services

External services have their own privacy rules. Diploma Notes links to or uses Firebase/Google, SITTTR Kerala, Google Drive where study files are hosted, Instagram where social links are provided, and external font providers where fonts are loaded.
