Privacy Policy

This page describes the information handled by the public study website, accounts, quizzes, Ask POLY AI and Help discussion.

Public study pages

Most lessons, department pages, syllabus links, notes links and tools can be opened without an account. Normal hosting and security logs may contain IP address, browser information, requested URL, date and time. These logs are used for operation, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

Supabase accounts and quiz records

Mock Exams use Supabase authentication. Registration and login may process an email address, account identifier, username and authentication metadata. Signed-in quiz use may store the user ID, subject and paper codes, selected answers, scores, feedback and timestamps. Daily Quiz scores are personal browser-calculated practice records and are not official or proctored results. Verified mock-exam scores may be stored only after server-side evaluation.

Row-level security is intended to restrict account records and results to the signed-in user. Do not reuse an important password from another service.

Ask POLY AI

When a question is sent to Ask POLY AI, the prompt, recent conversation context and any matched POLY PMNA website context may be sent through the configured Supabase relay or Cloudflare Worker and then to an available AI provider. Providers may include Cloudflare Workers AI, OpenAI, NVIDIA or Google depending on server configuration and availability. Do not enter passwords, private IDs, health information, financial information or other sensitive personal data.

Saved Ask POLY conversations are stored in the current browser using IndexedDB. They are not automatically synchronized between devices. Clearing site data or deleting a saved chat removes that browser copy.

Help discussion

The Help page may use Firebase anonymous authentication and Cloud Firestore for public comments and replies. A display name, comment text, anonymous user ID and timestamps may be stored and shown publicly. The browser may save the chosen display name locally. Deleted top-level comments may leave a placeholder so existing replies remain understandable.

Browser storage

LocalStorage and IndexedDB may be used for guest quiz history, unfinished answers, saved chats, tool favourites, recent tools, interface preferences and temporary status information. This information stays in the browser unless a feature explicitly submits it to an online service.

Third-party services

POLY PMNA may use or link to Supabase, Cloudflare, Firebase/Google, AI providers, SITTTR Kerala, Google Drive, GitHub, Instagram and external font or content services. Those services apply their own privacy terms when contacted.

Retention and deletion

Browser-only information can be removed by deleting the relevant chat, result or browser site data. Public Help content can be removed using the available delete control where ownership can be verified. Account and stored-result deletion requests can be submitted through the Help contact. Some operational logs and backups may remain temporarily where required for security, recovery or legal obligations.

Students and minors

This service is intended for educational use. Students should avoid sharing personal details. Where local law or institutional policy requires guardian or school approval for an online account, obtain that approval before registration.

Privacy questions

Contact POLY PMNA for access, correction or deletion questions. Include only the minimum information needed to identify the relevant account or content.
