Privacy Policy

This page describes the information handled by the public study website, accounts, quizzes, Ask POLY AI and Help discussion.

Public study pages

Most lessons, department pages, syllabus links, notes links and tools can be opened without an account. Normal hosting and security logs may contain IP address, browser information, requested URL, date and time. These logs are used for operation, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

Supabase accounts and quiz records

Mock Exams use Supabase authentication. Registration and login may process an email address, account identifier, username and authentication metadata. Signed-in quiz use may store the user ID, subject and paper codes, selected answers, scores, feedback and timestamps. Daily Quiz scores are personal browser-calculated practice records and are not official or proctored results. Verified mock-exam scores may be stored only after server-side evaluation.

Row-level security is intended to restrict account records and results to the signed-in user. Do not reuse an important password from another service.

Ask POLY AI

When a question is sent to Ask POLY AI, the prompt, recent conversation context and any matched POLY PMNA website context are sent to the POLY PMNA AI relay at api.polypmna.dpdns.org. The relay forwards the request to the currently configured AI provider for an answer. The active provider and configuration can change; the site does not promise that a particular provider will always be used. Do not enter passwords, private IDs, health information, financial information or other sensitive personal data.

The relay may process the submitted prompt, conversation context, matched website context, request time, technical logs and abuse-prevention metadata. Ask POLY conversations are saved locally in the current browser using IndexedDB and are not automatically synchronized between devices. The website does not intentionally store browser-saved chats on its own servers. Clearing site data or deleting a saved chat removes that browser copy; relay and provider operational logs may remain temporarily under the provider's retention and security policies.

Prompts are submitted to answer the request and are not intentionally used by POLY PMNA to train an AI model. Because the configured provider may change, users should review the provider's current privacy terms before submitting anything sensitive.

Help discussion

The Help page uses Firebase anonymous authentication and Cloud Firestore for public comments and replies. A display name, comment text, anonymous user ID and timestamps may be stored and shown publicly. The browser may save the chosen display name locally. Deleted top-level comments may leave a placeholder so existing replies remain understandable. Do not publish phone numbers, email addresses, passwords, student IDs or other personal information in a public comment.

Browser storage

LocalStorage and IndexedDB may be used for guest quiz history, unfinished answers, saved chats, tool favourites, recent tools, interface preferences and temporary status information. This information stays in the browser unless a feature explicitly submits it to an online service.

Third-party services

POLY PMNA currently uses or links to Supabase for account and quiz features, Cloudflare for delivery and the AI relay, Firebase/Google for Help discussion, SITTTR Kerala for official model-question papers, GitHub for selected syllabus files and the Android release, Instagram for the project profile, Google Fonts, and other explicitly listed external resources. These services may receive a browser request when their feature is opened. Each service applies its own privacy and retention terms when contacted.

For a current data-flow summary: public study pages are delivered by the website host; account and signed-in quiz data use Supabase; Ask POLY AI prompts use the POLY PMNA relay and its configured AI provider; Help comments use Firebase/Firestore; browser-only chats, guest quiz history and interface preferences remain in local browser storage unless a feature explicitly submits them online.

Retention and deletion

Browser-only information can be removed by deleting the relevant chat, result or browser site data. Public Help content can be removed using the available delete control where ownership can be verified. Account and stored-result deletion requests can be submitted through the Help contact. Ask POLY AI relay and provider logs are controlled by the configured infrastructure and may remain temporarily for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting or legal obligations; include only the minimum information needed in a request so that no sensitive data is unnecessarily transmitted.

Students and minors

This service is intended for educational use. Students should avoid sharing personal details. Where local law or institutional policy requires guardian or school approval for an online account, obtain that approval before registration.

Privacy questions

Contact POLY PMNA for access, correction or deletion questions. Include only the minimum information needed to identify the relevant account or content.
