Privacy

How the study website and Help discussion handle information.

Reading the study website

The main POLY PMNA study pages are publicly readable. You do not need an account to read lessons, open syllabus links, view notes links, or use the subject browsers.

Help discussion

The Help page uses Firebase anonymous authentication and Cloud Firestore for public comments and replies. When you post, the public display name you enter, your comment or reply text, an anonymous Firebase user ID, and timestamps may be stored. The browser may also save your chosen display name in localStorage so you do not need to type it again.

Top-level comments are soft-deleted when the author deletes them, so replies can remain understandable. Replies posted by the same anonymous user can be deleted by that user. Deleted comments may still leave a public placeholder such as “This comment was deleted.”

Do not share private details

Do not post passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, private IDs, account details, or sensitive personal information in the public discussion.

External services

External services have their own privacy rules. POLY PMNA links to or uses Firebase/Google, SITTTR Kerala, Google Drive where study files are hosted, Instagram where social links are provided, and external font providers where fonts are loaded.

Privacy questions

Email POLY PMNA for privacy-related questions.
