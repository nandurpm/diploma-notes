Course Overview

What this subject teaches

Power electronics is the engineering area where semiconductor switches control electrical power. For an electrical drawing/design engineer, this subject explains what is inside drives, UPS units, SMPS cards, battery chargers, soft starters and control panels.

Main purpose

Understand power devices and converters enough to identify circuits, read block diagrams, choose correct components and explain controlled power conversion.

Industrial value

Modern machines use power electronics for speed control, voltage regulation, soft starting, energy saving, regenerative braking and backup supply.

Escalator connection

Escalator systems use SMPS for controller supplies, UPS/battery backup, VFD/inverter drives, braking control and protection circuits.

No-nonsense warning: Do not study this subject by memorizing only definitions. If you cannot draw the basic circuit and trace current path during ON/OFF intervals, your practical understanding will be weak.

Module 1 - Power Semiconductor Devices and SCR

Select appropriate power semiconductor device for a particular application. Total syllabus load: 16 hours.

What you must understand

This module is the foundation. Before studying converters, the student must know what each power device can control, how it turns ON, how it turns OFF, voltage/current capability, datasheet meaning and protection.

ഈ module-ൽ power devices തമ്മിലുള്ള difference മനസ്സിലാക്കണം. SCR, TRIAC, MOSFET, IGBT എന്നിവയുടെ ON/OFF control, rating, protection എന്നിവ clear ആയാൽ rectifier, chopper, inverter പഠിക്കാൻ easy ആകും.

Textbook Notes

For exams, draw symbol/circuit first, write working sequence, then applications and limitations.

MOSFET

Voltage-controlled majority-carrier device. Gate current is almost zero in steady state. Fast switching makes it suitable for SMPS, low-to-medium voltage DC converters and high-frequency drives.

IGBT

Voltage-controlled device combining MOSFET gate control and BJT-like conduction. It is used in medium/high power inverters, UPS, industrial drives and traction systems.

UJT, DIAC and TRIAC

UJT is used in relaxation oscillators for SCR triggering. DIAC is a bidirectional trigger diode. TRIAC is a bidirectional thyristor used for AC power control.

SCR

Four-layer PNPN controlled rectifier. It turns ON by gate pulse or forward breakover and remains ON until current falls below holding current or forced commutation is applied.

Holding and latching current

Holding current is the minimum current required to keep SCR ON. Latching current is the minimum current required immediately after triggering so SCR remains ON after gate signal is removed.

SCR protection

Snubber circuit limits dv/dt. Series inductance or proper triggering limits di/dt. Heat sink protects junction temperature. Fuse/MCB protects from fault current.

Applications

  • Battery charger using SCR
  • SMPS using MOSFET
  • UPS/inverter using MOSFET or IGBT
  • Industrial motor drive using IGBT
  • AC light dimmer/heater control using TRIAC
  • Escalator drive inverter and braking circuits

Common mistakes

  • Thinking SCR can be turned OFF by removing gate signal.
  • Using MOSFET/IGBT without checking voltage, current, frequency and heat rating.
  • Ignoring dv/dt and di/dt protection in thyristor circuits.
  • Confusing latching current with holding current.

Module 2 - Controlled Rectifiers

Summarize operation of controlled rectifiers. Total syllabus load: 14 hours + 1 series test.

Core idea

Controlled rectifiers convert AC to controlled DC using SCRs. Average DC output voltage is controlled by delaying firing angle alpha.

Controlled rectifier AC voltage നെ DC ആക്കുന്നു. SCR firing angle മാറ്റിയാൽ output DC voltage control ചെയ്യാം. firing angle കൂടുമ്പോൾ average output voltage കുറയും.

Firing angle

Delay angle measured from natural turn-on point to gate pulse. Increasing alpha reduces average output voltage.

Freewheeling diode

Provides path for inductive load current when SCR is OFF or reverse biased, improving load current continuity.

Half wave controlled rectifier

One SCR in series with load. During positive half cycle SCR conducts only after gate pulse at alpha and turns OFF at natural current zero for R load.

Full wave bridge controlled rectifier

Four SCR bridge. Diagonal pairs conduct in alternate half cycles. Suitable for controlled DC output with lower ripple than half wave.

Semi-controlled bridge

Uses two SCRs and two diodes. It is cheaper and simpler than full converter but cannot operate as inverter.

Three phase bridge converter

Uses six devices and gives smoother DC output. Syllabus needs basic circuit and concept for resistive load.

CO3 • Choppers • Buck • Boost • Cycloconverter

Module 3 - DC and AC Converters

Summarize DC choppers, buck/boost converters and cycloconverters. Total: 12 hours.

Core idea

A DC chopper converts fixed DC into variable DC by high-speed switching. Buck and boost converters are practical chopper-based DC-DC converters. Cycloconverter performs direct AC-AC frequency conversion.

Chopper ഒരു DC switch പോലെ ആണ്. ON time/OFF time control ചെയ്താൽ average output voltage മാറും. Duty cycle ആണ് main control variable.

Duty cycle

D = Ton / T, where T = Ton + Toff. For step-down chopper, Vo = DVs.

Control strategies

Constant frequency control keeps T constant and varies Ton. Variable frequency control varies switching frequency.

Chopper types

Type A - first quadrant. Type B - second quadrant. Type C - first/second. Type D - first/fourth. Type E - all four quadrants.

Buck converter

Step-down converter using switch, diode, inductor and capacitor. Ideal continuous conduction output is Vo = DVs.

Boost converter

Step-up converter. Ideal continuous conduction output is Vo = Vs/(1-D). Inductor stores energy during switch ON and releases during switch OFF.

Cycloconverter

Converts fixed-frequency AC directly into variable-frequency AC. Single phase midpoint type is studied at concept level.

Module 4 - Inverters, SMPS, UPS and Electric Drives

Summarize inverters and electric drives. Total syllabus load: 16 hours + 1 series test.

Core idea

An inverter converts DC to AC. Modern inverters are the heart of UPS systems, solar systems, VFDs and motor control panels.

Inverter DC-യെ AC ആക്കുന്നു. VFD drive-ൽ rectifier DC link ഉണ്ടാക്കി, inverter output frequency/voltage control ചെയ്ത് motor speed control ചെയ്യുന്നു.

CSI and VSI

CSI has stiff DC current source; VSI has stiff DC voltage source. Modern drives commonly use VSI with IGBT switches.

Bridge inverter

Single phase full bridge uses four switches. Diagonal pairs conduct alternately to produce AC output.

Feedback diodes

Provide path for reactive current in inductive loads and return energy to DC source.

PWM

Pulse width modulation controls inverter output voltage and harmonic content. Sinusoidal PWM compares sine reference with triangular carrier.

SMPS and stabilizer

SMPS uses high-frequency switching for efficient regulated DC. Servo stabilizer uses sensing and servo-controlled voltage correction.

UPS and drives

UPS may be offline, online or line-interactive. Electric drive includes source, converter, motor, controller, sensor and load.

Formula Bank

Equations to remember

SCR and rectifier

Conduction angle = turn-off angle - firing angle
Latching current > Holding current
Half-wave controlled rectifier, R load: Vdc = (Vm/2π)(1 + cos α)
Full converter average output: Vdc = (2Vm/π) cos α

Chopper and converters

T = Ton + Toff
Duty cycle D = Ton / T
Step-down chopper / buck: Vo = DVs
Boost converter: Vo = Vs / (1 - D)
Solved Examples

Worked problems and answer models

Example 1 - Step-down chopper output voltage

Input voltage 220 V, Ton = 3 ms, Toff = 2 ms. T = 5 ms. D = 3/5 = 0.6. Vo = DVs = 0.6 × 220 = 132 V.

Example 2 - Boost converter output

Input 24 V and duty cycle 0.4. Vo = Vs/(1-D) = 24/0.6 = 40 V.

Example 3 - SCR firing angle effect

Increasing firing angle delays SCR conduction. The output waveform area reduces, so average DC output voltage decreases.

Example 4 - Device selection

For high-frequency 24 V SMPS, MOSFET is normally selected because it is voltage controlled and fast. For medium/high power motor drives, IGBT is usually better.

Rectifier

Answer Key

Complete model answers - condensed exam format

1. Holding current and latching current

Holding current is the minimum anode current required to keep an SCR conducting. Latching current is the minimum current required immediately after triggering to keep SCR ON after gate pulse is removed.

2. Applications of IGBT

IGBT is used in VFDs, UPS inverters, solar inverters, induction heating, welding equipment, traction drives and industrial motor control.

3. Freewheeling diode

It is connected across inductive load to provide path for load current when SCR is OFF or reverse biased.

4. Firing angle and conduction angle

Firing angle is the delay angle from natural turn-on point to gate triggering. Conduction angle is the angular period for which the device conducts.

5. Duty cycle

D = Ton / T, where T = Ton + Toff. For step-down chopper, Vo = DVs.

6. Quadrant operation

It indicates the sign of output voltage and current. Type A: first quadrant. Type B: second. Type C: first and second. Type D: first and fourth. Type E: all four.

7. Practical inverter requirements

Required voltage/frequency, low harmonics, high efficiency, overload protection, thermal protection, stable control and reliable switching.

8. UPS configurations

Offline UPS, online UPS and line-interactive UPS.

9. SMPS

Switched Mode Power Supply uses high-frequency switching, transformer/isolation if required, rectification, filtering and feedback to produce regulated DC efficiently.

10. Electric drive

Blocks: source, power converter, motor, mechanical load, controller, sensor/feedback and protection.

11. MOSFET

N-channel enhancement MOSFET has p-substrate, n+ source/drain and insulated gate. Positive VGS creates channel; drain current flows when VDS is applied. It is fast and voltage controlled.

12. SCR V-I characteristics

SCR has reverse blocking, forward blocking and conduction regions. Gate pulse moves it from forward blocking to ON state. It turns OFF when current falls below holding current.

13. SCR turn-ON methods

Gate triggering, forward voltage triggering, dv/dt triggering, temperature triggering and light triggering. Gate triggering is preferred.

14. Half wave controlled rectifier

One SCR is connected in series with load. During positive half cycle it is forward biased. At alpha, gate pulse turns it ON. It conducts from alpha to pi for R load.

15. Full wave bridge controlled rectifier

Four SCRs are connected as bridge. Diagonal pairs conduct alternately. RL load current may continue due to inductance. Freewheeling diode improves current path.

16. Type A and Type B choppers

Type A is first-quadrant motoring chopper. Type B is second-quadrant regenerative braking chopper.

17. Buck converter

Step-down DC converter using switch, diode, inductor and capacitor. During ON inductor stores energy; during OFF current flows through diode. Vo = DVs ideally.

18. Offline vs Online UPS

Offline UPS switches to inverter during mains failure and has transfer time. Online UPS continuously feeds load through inverter, giving better continuity and power quality.

19. IGBT

IGBT combines MOSFET gate control with BJT-like conduction. It has high input impedance and good current capability. Used in VFD, UPS, traction, welding and inverters.

20. SCR turn-off and snubber

SCR turns OFF when anode current falls below holding current. AC circuits use natural commutation; DC circuits use forced commutation. RC snubber limits dv/dt.

21. Semi-controlled rectifier

Uses two SCRs and two diodes. Firing angle controls positive DC output. It is suitable where only positive voltage control is required.

22. Chopper classification

Type A first quadrant, Type B second, Type C first/second, Type D first/fourth, Type E four-quadrant reversible operation.

23. Full bridge inverter

S1-S4 and S2-S3 conduct alternately. For RL load, feedback diodes conduct reactive current and return energy to DC source.

24. SMPS, stabilizer and UPS

SMPS uses high-frequency switching and feedback. Servo stabilizer senses output and corrects voltage using servo mechanism. UPS provides backup using rectifier, battery and inverter.
