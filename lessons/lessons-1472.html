Official module 1

Evolution and Fundamentals of Business

Business is an organised economic activity that creates and exchanges value. This module builds the vocabulary needed to understand why organisations exist, how they are classified, and how functions fit together.

Hours: 13
CO1 · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Define business and explain its features, importance, scope, and objectives.
  • Classify economic activities and business activities.
  • Identify components and basic business functions.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M1.01 — Evolution, definition, features, importance, scope and objectives of business (7 hours)

Concept and explanation

Business evolved from simple exchange to organised production, services, digital platforms, and global networks. It involves regular economic activity, production or procurement, sale or exchange, risk, and an objective of value creation or return. Its scope includes industry, trade, commerce, services, entrepreneurship, and support systems. Objectives may include survival, profit, growth, customer value, employment, innovation, and social responsibility.

Malayalam support: Business എന്നത് regular economic activity ആയി goods/services create ചെയ്ത് exchange ചെയ്യുന്ന സംവിധാനമാണ്. Profit മാത്രം ലക്ഷ്യം അല്ല; customer value, growth, employment എന്നിവയും പ്രധാനമാണ്.

Study points

  • Separate business from a one-time hobby sale; identify risk and continuity; relate scope to hospitality; distinguish objective from method.
Engineering / workplace application: Hotels create accommodation and food value; travel businesses coordinate services; technology supports booking and payment.
Illustrative example: A hotel’s room sale is business activity because it is organised, repeated, priced, and supported by service operations.
Common mistake: Profit is an objective, but not a complete definition of business; regular exchange and organised activity matter.
M1.02 — Classifications of economic activities, components and functions (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

Economic activities are broadly grouped into production/industry, trade, and services; business activities may also be classified by size, ownership, sector, or output. Components include resources, people, finance, technology, information, and processes. Functions include purchasing, production/operations, marketing, finance, accounting, human resources, and administration.

Malayalam support: Economic activity-കൾ production, trade, service എന്നീ രീതിയിൽ കാണാം. Business functions പരസ്പരം linked ആണ്; ഒരൊറ്റ function കൊണ്ട് organisation നടക്കില്ല.

Study points

  • Classify an example using more than one basis; map resources to functions; identify primary and support functions.
Engineering / workplace application: A hotel combines operations, marketing, human resources, finance, stores, housekeeping, and guest service.
Illustrative example: For a hotel, purchasing buys linen, operations uses it, stores controls it, accounts records cost, and marketing communicates service value.
Common mistake: Calling accounting and finance the same function in every context hides their different roles.
Business reasons — Reasons to start a business (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

The outline lists reasons to start a business under M1.02. Reasons can include identifying a customer need, using a skill or resource, earning income, creating employment, gaining independence, responding to opportunity, or solving a social or technical problem. A sound decision also examines demand, competition, resources, risk, legal requirements, and ethics.

Malayalam support: Business start ചെയ്യുന്നത് profit മാത്രം നോക്കി ആവരുത്. Need, resources, risk, competition, legal requirements എന്നിവയും പരിശോധിക്കണം.

Study points

  • Separate motivation from feasibility; write a simple problem-solution statement; identify stakeholders and risks.
Engineering / workplace application: A small homestay or catering service may begin by responding to a local travel or event need.
Illustrative example: Before opening a café, estimate target customers, menu cost, location, staff, permits, and break-even needs.
Common mistake: An attractive idea without demand and resource planning is not automatically a viable business.
Module summary: Business fundamentals explain what an organisation does, why it exists, and how its functions connect.
Official module 2

Ownership, Economics and Communication

Ownership determines control, risk, capital, and legal responsibility. Economics explains choices under scarcity, while communication coordinates people and decisions inside the organisation.

Hours: 10
CO2 · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Compare sole proprietorship, partnership, collaborations, LLC, mergers, acquisitions, alliances, joint ventures, and consolidations.
  • Define economics and relate it to business choices.
  • Explain the importance of communication in business.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M2.01 — Business ownerships and combinations (4 hours)

Concept and explanation

Sole proprietorship is owned and controlled by one person; partnership distributes ownership and responsibility among partners. A limited liability company separates the business entity and limits liability according to law. Collaborations, strategic alliances, joint ventures, mergers, acquisitions, and consolidations are ways organisations share capability, enter markets, combine operations, or change control. The legal meaning varies by jurisdiction and must be checked before action.

Malayalam support: Sole, partnership, LLC എന്നിവ ownership forms ആണ്. Alliance/joint venture/merger/acquisition എന്നിവ organisations തമ്മിലുള്ള strategic combinations ആണ്.

Study points

  • Compare ownership by control, capital, liability, continuity, and decision speed; distinguish collaboration from acquisition; use local law as final authority.
Engineering / workplace application: Hotels may partner with brands, merge operations, or form a joint venture for a new property.
Illustrative example: A joint venture creates a shared project or entity, while an acquisition transfers control of one business to another.
Common mistake: Treating every alliance as a merger is incorrect; an alliance may preserve separate ownership.
M2.02 — Economics and its relation to business (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

Economics studies how scarce resources are allocated to satisfy wants. Business decisions involve demand, supply, price, cost, revenue, competition, opportunity cost, and productivity. Managers use economic reasoning to decide what to produce, how much capacity to operate, and how to respond to changes.

Malayalam support: Resources limited ആണ്; wants കൂടുതലാണ്. Business-ൽ cost, demand, price, opportunity cost എന്നിവ ഉപയോഗിച്ച് choices എടുക്കണം.

Study points

  • Identify scarce resources; distinguish fixed and variable costs conceptually; connect demand to pricing; state assumptions before making a decision.
Engineering / workplace application: Hotel room pricing responds to season, demand, capacity, competition, and service cost.
Illustrative example: If an extra room can be sold during peak demand but requires additional staff, the manager compares incremental revenue with incremental cost.
Common mistake: Reducing price always increases profit is false; demand response and cost must be considered.
Communication — Importance of communication in business (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

Business communication transfers information, instructions, decisions, feedback, and records between people and departments. It may be oral, written, visual, digital, formal, or informal. Effective communication is clear, complete, timely, respectful, audience-appropriate, and confirmed when the matter is important.

Malayalam support: Communication clear, complete, timely ആയിരിക്കണം. Hotel-ൽ guest request തെറ്റാതെ pass ചെയ്യാൻ listening, confirmation, record എന്നിവ ആവശ്യമാണ്.

Study points

  • Choose the channel to match urgency; use objective language; confirm critical details; protect confidential information; keep written evidence when required.
Engineering / workplace application: Reservation details, shift handover, purchase orders, and complaint handling require reliable communication.
Illustrative example: A good handover states room number, request, deadline, responsible person, and completion confirmation.
Common mistake: Passing a vague verbal message such as “guest needs something soon” causes avoidable service failure.
Module summary: Ownership, economic choice, and communication explain how business decisions are controlled and coordinated.
Official module 3

Management Levels and Business Functions

Management converts organisational resources into coordinated results. Different levels focus on different time horizons, but all depend on planning, communication, and responsible decision-making.

Hours: 10
CO3 · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Explain management levels and functions.
  • Identify basic managerial skills and roles.
  • Describe marketing, operations, employee motivation, accounting, and finance.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M3.01 — Levels of management, functions, skills and roles (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

Top or strategic management sets direction and long-term policy; middle management converts strategy into departmental plans; supervisory or first-line management guides daily work. Core functions include planning, organising, staffing, directing, coordinating, and controlling. Skills include technical, human, conceptual, communication, problem-solving, and decision skills.

Malayalam support: Top, middle, first-line management-ുകൾക്ക് different time horizons ഉണ്ട്. Planning, organising, staffing, directing, controlling എന്നിവ management functions ആണ്.

Study points

  • Match a decision to its likely level; distinguish role from skill; use measurable objectives; link control with feedback.
Engineering / workplace application: A hotel general manager plans strategy, a department manager schedules resources, and a supervisor checks shift execution.
Illustrative example: A supervisor who notices a shortage, reallocates staff, records the cause, and follows up is performing management control.
Common mistake: Management is not only giving orders; it includes planning, support, measurement, and correction.
M3.02 — Marketing, operations, motivation, accounting and finance (4 hours)

Concept and explanation

Marketing identifies needs, creates value, communicates the offer, and supports exchange. Operations transforms inputs into goods or services. Motivation encourages employees through meaningful work, recognition, fair conditions, growth, and leadership. Accounting records and reports financial transactions; finance plans, obtains, and uses funds. These functions are interdependent.

Malayalam support: Marketing customer value communicate ചെയ്യുന്നു; operations service deliver ചെയ്യുന്നു; motivation people-നെ engage ചെയ്യുന്നു; accounting record ചെയ്യുന്നു; finance funds manage ചെയ്യുന്നു.

Study points

  • Map a hotel activity to all five functions; distinguish accounting records from finance decisions; connect employee experience to guest experience.
Engineering / workplace application: A hotel promotion affects demand, operations must deliver the promise, accounts track cost, and finance evaluates return.
Illustrative example: A “weekend package” succeeds only when marketing promise, room inventory, kitchen capacity, staffing, billing, and feedback align.
Common mistake: Marketing a service that operations cannot deliver damages trust.
Module summary: Management levels divide attention; business functions cooperate to create and control value.
Official module 4

Hotel Business: History, Categories and Ownership

Hotels are businesses with a long history of hosting travellers, but their categories and ownership forms continue to evolve with transport, tourism, technology, and guest expectations.

Hours: 5
CO4 · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Trace broad history and evolution of hotel business.
  • Classify hotels by service, size, location, clientele, and standard.
  • Identify types of hotel ownership.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M4.01 — History, evolution, categories, classifications and ownership (5 hours)

Concept and explanation

Hospitality evolved from inns and traveller accommodation to organised hotels, resorts, branded chains, boutique properties, serviced apartments, hostels, homestays, and technology-enabled lodging. Hotels may be classified by location, size, star or service standard, clientele, duration of stay, meal plan, or ownership. Ownership may be independent, leased, franchised, managed, or chain-based.

Malayalam support: Hotel business travel, tourism, transport, technology എന്നിവയോടൊപ്പം evolve ചെയ്തു. Classification പല basis-ൽ ചെയ്യാം; ownership operation-നെ ബാധിക്കും.

Study points

  • Use the stated basis when classifying; distinguish ownership from management contract; compare standardisation with independence.
Engineering / workplace application: A franchise may use a brand system while the property is owned by another entity; a management contract separates operation from ownership.
Illustrative example: A resort may be independently owned but professionally managed under a contract.
Common mistake: Brand name alone does not prove who owns the property.
Module summary: Hotel categories help managers match product, market, ownership, and service standards.
Official module 5

Hotel Management and Operations

Hotel management integrates people, facilities, service standards, technology, finance, and guest relationships. Its necessity becomes clear when many departments must deliver one consistent experience.

Hours: 7
CO5 · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Define hotel management and explain its importance.
  • Identify types, skills, strategies, and common mistakes.
  • Relate technology to hotel operations.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

M5.01 — Definition, importance, parts, types, skills, strategies, mistakes and technology in hotel management (7 hours)

Concept and explanation

Hotel management plans and controls the operation of accommodation and related services. Parts may include front office, housekeeping, food and beverage, kitchen, engineering, security, human resources, sales, accounts, and stores. Skills include leadership, service recovery, planning, communication, cost control, technology use, and ethical judgement. Strategies should align guest value, staff capability, quality, and financial sustainability.

Malayalam support: Hotel management എല്ലാം departments-നെ coordinate ചെയ്ത് consistent guest experience നൽകുന്നു. Leadership, planning, service recovery, cost control എന്നിവ പ്രധാന skills ആണ്.

Study points

  • Map departments and handoffs; identify a service standard; use technology as support rather than replacement for judgement; learn from complaints.
Engineering / workplace application: Property-management systems, online distribution, digital payments, housekeeping apps, and guest feedback systems support operations.
Illustrative example: A digital room-status update helps front office and housekeeping coordinate, but staff still verify the room before release.
Common mistake: Technology cannot correct unclear responsibility or poor service culture.
Module summary: Hotel management is coordinated service leadership supported by systems, people, and technology.
