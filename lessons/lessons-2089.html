Module 1: Static Characteristics & Galvanometer Conversion

12 Hours
M1.01: Static Characteristics & Calibration

Static characteristics are the properties of an instrument when the input is either constant or varying very slowly.

  • Accuracy: The degree of closeness to the true value.
  • Precision: The degree of repeatability of measurements.
  • Error: The difference between measured value and true value. ($Error = A_m - A_t$)
  • Sensitivity: The ratio of change in output to the change in input.
  • Hysteresis: The difference in readings when a value is approached from an increasing direction versus a decreasing direction.

M1.02: Galvanometer Conversion

A D'Arsonval galvanometer can be converted into an Ammeter or Voltmeter by adding specific resistances.

1. Conversion to Ammeter

A low resistance called Shunt ($R_s$) is connected in parallel with the galvanometer.

Rs = Rg / (n - 1)

Where $n = I / I_g$ (Multiplying factor), $I$ is the total current to be measured, and $I_g$ is full-scale deflection current.

2. Conversion to Voltmeter

A high resistance called Multiplier ($R_m$) is connected in series with the galvanometer.

Rm = Rg (n - 1)

Where $n = V / V_g$ (Multiplying factor), $V$ is the total voltage to be measured.

Module 2: Measurement using Bridges

8 Hours
M2.01: Wheatstone’s Bridge (Resistance)

Used for measuring medium resistances (1Ω to 100kΩ). It consists of four arms, a DC source, and a galvanometer.

Balance Condition: $R_x = (R_2 / R_1) \times R_3$

M2.02: Hay’s Bridge (Inductance)

Specifically used for measuring high-Q inductors (Q > 10). It differs from Maxwell's bridge as the capacitor and resistor are in series.

M2.03: Schering Bridge (Capacitance)

Used for measuring unknown capacitance, dielectric loss, and power factor. It is one of the most important AC bridges.

Module 3: Calibration of Wattmeter & Energy Meter

8 Hours

Direct Loading Method

In this method, the meter is connected to a real load (like a lamp bank or rheostat). The reading of the meter under test is compared with a standard (calibrated) meter.

Percentage Error Calculation:
% Error = [(Measured Value - True Value) / True Value] × 100
Energy Meter Calibration: Energy meter കറങ്ങുന്ന ഡിസ്കിന്റെ എണ്ണം (Revolutions) നോക്കിയാണ് സാധാരണയായി കാലിബ്രേറ്റ് ചെയ്യുന്നത്. ഇതിനായി സ്റ്റോപ്പ് വാച്ച് ഉപയോഗിച്ച് സമയം അളക്കുന്നു.

Module 4: Oscilloscopes & Recorders

14 Hours
M4.01 - M4.03: CRO and DSO Applications

CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope) and DSO (Digital Storage Oscilloscope) are used to visualize waveforms.

  • Voltage Measurement: $V_{p-p} = (\text{Vertical divisions}) \times (\text{Volts/div setting})$
  • Time Period: $T = (\text{Horizontal divisions for 1 cycle}) \times (\text{Time/div setting})$
  • Frequency: $f = 1/T$

Lissajous Patterns

Used for frequency and phase measurement by applying two different signals to the X and Y plates.

Pattern Shape Phase Difference (φ)
Straight Line (Diagonal)0° or 360°
Circle90° or 270°
EllipseBetween 0° and 90°
M4.04: Strip Chart Recorder

A device that records a continuous visual record of a signal on a moving strip of paper.

  • Drive Mechanism: Moves the paper at a constant speed.
  • Stylus/Pen: Moves across the paper proportional to the input signal.

Practice Questions

1. Define Hysteresis in instruments.
Hysteresis is the maximum difference in output readings for the same input, when the input is approached from opposite directions (increasing and decreasing).
2. Why is a shunt used in an ammeter?
A shunt is used to bypass the majority of the current around the galvanometer coil, allowing the meter to measure currents much larger than its full-scale deflection current without damaging the coil.
3. Which bridge is best for measuring high-Q inductance?
Hay's Bridge is preferred for high-Q inductors (Q > 10).

Quick Revision

  • Static Characteristics: Accuracy, Precision, Sensitivity, Hysteresis, Resolution.
  • Ammeter: Low resistance shunt in parallel.
  • Voltmeter: High resistance multiplier in series.
  • Wheatstone Bridge: Measures medium resistance.
  • Schering Bridge: Measures capacitance.
  • Hay's Bridge: Measures high-Q inductance.
  • CRO: Measures Voltage, Time, Frequency, and Phase.

Glossary

DSO
Digital Storage Oscilloscope - An oscilloscope that digitizes and stores the input signal.
Loading Effect
The distortion of the signal being measured caused by the act of connecting the measuring instrument.
Q-Factor
Quality factor; a dimensionless parameter that describes how underdamped an oscillator or resonator is.
