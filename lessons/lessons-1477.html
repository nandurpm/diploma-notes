Official module 1

Kitchen Equipment, Ingredients and Practical Menus

This practical course develops confidence through repeated mise-en-place, controlled cooking, presentation, and cleaning. Each menu should be treated as a complete production cycle.

Hours: 45
CO1 · Bloom level: Understanding

Learning goals

  • Identify equipment and ingredients before starting.
  • Perform the listed salad, cake, dessert, bread, stock, sauce, vegetable, meat, fish, and egg preparations.
  • Present the dish and evaluate quality, safety, and cleanliness.

Module study tip

Read every topic in sequence, reproduce its example or procedure, and write a short explanation before attempting the module questions.

P1.01 — Familiarization of kitchen equipment and standard cuts of vegetable (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

Begin by identifying the work surface, knives, boards, pans, bowls, scales, heat source, and cleaning equipment. Demonstrate standard vegetable cuts with stable posture, controlled knife movement, uniform size, and safe storage. The cut should match the recipe and desired cooking time.

Malayalam support: Equipment-ും ingredient-ും ആദ്യം identify ചെയ്യണം. Knife handling, board stability, uniform cut, cleaning എന്നിവ പ്രധാനമാണ്.

Study points

  • Name equipment; inspect condition; wash and prepare ingredients; use colour-coded or institution-prescribed boards; record cut size and yield.
Engineering / workplace application: Uniform cuts cook evenly and improve plating in every menu.
Illustrative example: Prepare a mise-en-place list, demonstrate the cut, collect trim for approved stock or waste control, and clean the station.
Common mistake: Starting a practical without checking equipment and ingredients creates delay and unsafe substitutions.
Menu 1 — Simple salads: coleslaw, potato, beetroot, green and fruit salad (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

Salad preparation requires clean ingredients, appropriate cuts, drained components, balanced seasoning, and correct dressing. Coleslaw balances shredded vegetables with dressing; potato salad depends on cooked but firm potato; beetroot needs colour control; green salad must remain crisp; fruit salad requires freshness and careful handling.

Malayalam support: Salad-ൽ ingredients clean, chilled, drained ആയിരിക്കണം. Dressing balanceയും textureയും പ്രധാനമാണ്.

Study points

  • Prepare ingredients separately; season near service; maintain colour and crispness; portion and garnish consistently.
Engineering / workplace application: Salads demonstrate knife skill, cold-food hygiene, colour, texture, and portion control.
Common mistake: Overcooking potato or dressing green salad too early causes poor texture.
Menu 2 — Simple cakes: enriched sponge, fatless Swiss roll, Madeira and fruit-rich cakes (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

Cake quality depends on accurate weighing, ingredient temperature, mixing method, air incorporation, pan preparation, and controlled baking. Enriched sponge includes added fat or richness; a fatless sponge relies on aerated eggs; Swiss roll needs flexible sheet and correct rolling; Madeira and fruit-rich cakes need suitable mixing and baking.

Malayalam support: Baking-ൽ weighing, mixing method, aeration, pan preparation, oven temperature വളരെ പ്രധാനമാണ്.

Study points

  • Preheat; measure accurately; avoid overmixing; test doneness; cool before finishing; record yield and defects.
Engineering / workplace application: Cakes teach raising, structure, moisture, and heat control.
Common mistake: Opening the oven repeatedly or overmixing an aerated batter can collapse the product.
Menu 3 — Hot and cold desserts (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

The listed desserts are caramel custard, lemon or pineapple soufflé, chocolate or coffee mousse, and puddings. These demonstrate custard setting, foaming, aeration, chilling, moulding, and controlled heat. Texture and temperature at service are part of quality.

Malayalam support: Dessert-ുകളിൽ setting, foam, chilling, moulding, temperature control എന്നിവ മനസ്സിലാക്കണം.

Study points

  • Use clean whisk and bowl; control egg heat; chill for required time; portion and present neatly; record texture.
Engineering / workplace application: Desserts develop precision and presentation skills.
Common mistake: High heat curdles custard; excess handling collapses soufflé or mousse.
Menu 4 — Bread rolls, croutons, bread sticks, stocks, tossed/glazed vegetables and sauces (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

The syllabus combines bread rolls, croutons, bread sticks, white/brown/fish stock, tossed and glazed vegetables, white sauce, brown sauce, mayonnaise, velouté, and hollandaise. The practical sequence should separate dough preparation, stock simmering, vegetable finishing, and sauce control.

Malayalam support: Bread, stock, vegetable, sauce preparations-ൽ timing, temperature, consistency, hygiene എന്നിവ coordinate ചെയ്യണം.

Study points

  • Prepare mise-en-place for each group; label stock; control sauce heat; taste and adjust; plate each item.
Engineering / workplace application: This menu integrates core food-production skills across baking, liquid extraction, vegetables, emulsions, and sauces.
Common mistake: Leaving stock uncovered or overheating hollandaise damages quality and safety.
Menu 5 — Roast chicken, roasted potatoes, cream soup, pasta in cream sauce, fish gratin and lemon jelly (6 hours)

Concept and explanation

This menu combines dry heat, starch preparation, soup consistency, cream sauce, gratin finishing, fish cookery, and gel setting. Sequence tasks by cooking time: start long processes first, maintain safe holding, and finish delicate items close to service.

Malayalam support: Long-cooking items ആദ്യം തുടങ്ങണം; fish, cream sauce, jelly എന്നിവ timing-sensitive ആണ്.

Study points

  • Plan production chart; verify doneness; avoid cross-contamination; control cream temperature; present hot and cold items appropriately.
Engineering / workplace application: A menu plan demonstrates how several dishes reach service quality together.
Common mistake: Cooking everything at one time without a production schedule causes overcooked or cold dishes.
Menu 6 — Repeated cake and dessert preparations (9 hours)

Concept and explanation

Menu 6 repeats simple cake preparations—enriched sponge, fatless Swiss rolls, Madeira, fruit-rich cakes—and hot/cold desserts—caramel custard, soufflé, mousse, and puddings. The repetition is useful for improving consistency, timing, presentation, and diagnosis of defects.

Malayalam support: Menu 6-ൽ repeat practice വഴി consistency മെച്ചപ്പെടുത്തണം. Previous defects note ചെയ്ത് correction ചെയ്യുക.

Study points

  • Compare batch results; record weights, oven setting, time, texture, and presentation; identify one improvement per repetition.
Engineering / workplace application: Repeated production mirrors professional standardisation and recipe testing.
Common mistake: Changing the recipe without recording the change makes comparison impossible.
Module summary: Practical competence is demonstrated by safe preparation, controlled method, consistent output, and a clean workstation.
