# Required Formula Bank for 5032

The lesson must include at least these formulas:

1. Hydel power: `P = 9.81 Q H η kW`
2. Energy generated: `Energy = Power × Time`
3. Average demand: `Total units / Time`
4. Load factor: `Average demand / Maximum demand`
5. Demand factor: `Maximum demand / Connected load`
6. Diversity factor: `Sum of individual maximum demands / Maximum demand of system`
7. Capacitor kVAr: `Qc = P(tanφ1 − tanφ2)`
8. Sag for equal-level supports: `y = wL² / 8T`
9. Transmission efficiency: `Receiving end power / Sending end power × 100%`
10. Voltage regulation formula
11. String efficiency formula
