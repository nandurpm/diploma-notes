Safety, ergonomics and responsible use

Workstation posture

  • Keep feet supported and shoulders relaxed.
  • Place the monitor roughly an arm's length away.
  • Keep the top of the screen near eye level.
  • Keep wrists neutral while typing.
  • Take short visual and movement breaks.

Electrical and equipment safety

  • Use the instructed power sequence and UPS.
  • Keep liquids away from equipment.
  • Do not insert damaged cables.
  • Do not open equipment without supervision.
  • Report heat, smell, sparks or damaged ports immediately.

Digital safety

  • Use strong unique passwords.
  • Do not save passwords on shared systems.
  • Scan removable drives where required.
  • Avoid unknown downloads and links.
  • Log out from email and cloud accounts.
