WHY WE EXIST

Bridging the gap between official syllabus and active study.

Navigating fragmented technical curriculums can be exhausting for polytechnic students. Information is often scattered across outdated lists, slow-loading portals, and confusing document formats. POLY PMNA was engineered to provide structured, mobile-responsive, and bilingual course catalogs that bring clarity to every semester.

💡

Revision 2026 is the current curriculum for all ongoing standard semesters, designed for modern engineering fields.

Core Philosophy

We believe learning resources must be robust, reliable, and instantaneous. By keeping legacy schemes completely separate from current ones, we prevent subject code mixups.

  • Active practice over plain reading
  • Verified sources of academic truth
  • Inclusive bilingual translations

One streamlined path from syllabus to practice.

How to study effectively with POLY PMNA

This interactive guide advances automatically. Pause, use controls, keyboard arrows, or swipe on mobile devices.

●Autoplay on · Guide step 1 of 6
Step 1 · Curriculum

Choose the correct revision first.

Always verify your batch. Revision 2026 is the current curriculum for ongoing classes, while Revision 2021 remains available as a legacy archive.

റീവിഷൻ മാറുമ്പോൾ സിലബസ്സ് ഘടനയും വിഷയങ്ങളും മാറാം.

Step 2 · Department

Select your programme and semester.

Navigate to your specific department page and scroll to your current semester. Common core subjects are visible across multiple directories.

ശരിയായ പേരും സെമസ്റ്റർ നമ്പറും പരിശോധിക്കുക.

Step 3 · Subject Code

Always verify the subject code.

Read the precise subject code displayed on each card. Cross-check modules, credits, and evaluation weights with the official SITTTR syllabus link.

പേരുകൾ ഒരുപോലെയാണെങ്കിലും കോഡുകൾ വ്യത്യസ്തമായിരിക്കാം.

Step 4 · Active Learning

Study with handbooks and notes.

Access structured module layouts, concise explanations, and high-fidelity diagrams. Use the Print option to save PDF copies for offline study.

പ്രധാന ആശയങ്ങൾ മലയാളത്തിലും സാങ്കേതിക വിവരങ്ങൾ ഇംഗ്ലീഷിലും നൽകിയിരിക്കുന്നു.

Step 5 · Practice Exams

Assess yourself with mock exams.

Attempt daily practice quizzes and full mock assessments. Identify gaps in your knowledge through instant performance breakdown.

തെറ്റുകൾ തിരുത്തി പഠിക്കുന്നതിന് മുൻഗണന നൽകുക.

Step 6 · Continuous Help

Ask the AI or report corrections.

Query Ask POLY AI for prompt resource directions or study explanations. If you notice any missing resource, report it using the Help form.

Development Journey & Future Roadmap

A timeline of our major milestones and what we plan next to support polytechnic students.

2021

Portal Conception & Legacy Revision 2021

Initially launched to support 43 department pages under the Revision 2021 syllabus, organizing directories by core subject cards and syllabus linkages.

2023

Interactive Quiz Platform & Student Tools

Introduced continuous daily practice quizzes and lightweight student tools including attendance and CGPA calculators.

2024

Ask POLY AI Integration

Deployed edge-computed AI study guides and search assistance using secure Supabase and Cloudflare relays.

2026

Curriculum Revision 2026 & Bilingual Support

Transitioned standard classes to Revision 2026 as the current curriculum, deploying complete bilingual Malayalam toggle and high-fidelity mobile-responsive layouts.

Future

Roadmap: Deeper Interactive Subject Coverage

Expanding printable study notes for all minor courses under Revision 2026, delivering enhanced visual simulators, and releasing progressive offline mobile apps.
