How to use POLY PMNA

The guide moves automatically every few seconds. Pause it, use the arrows, select a dot, swipe on mobile or use the keyboard.

●Autoplay on · Guide step 1 of 6
Step 1 · Revision

Choose the correct curriculum first.

Use Revision 2026 for the current curriculum. Open Revision 2021 or the 2015 archive only when your batch follows that scheme.

Revision മാറിയാൽ subject code, title, syllabus, exam pattern എന്നിവ മാറാം.

Step 2 · Programme

Open your department and semester.

Select the correct programme page, then move to the semester you are studying. Common subjects may appear across several departments.

Department പേരും semester നമ്പറും വീണ്ടും പരിശോധിക്കുക.

Step 3 · Subject

Verify the subject code before opening resources.

Read the subject title and code on the card. Use the official syllabus link to confirm modules, hours, marks and course outcomes.

Subject name മാത്രം നോക്കാതെ code കൂടി പരിശോധിക്കുക.

Step 4 · Learn

Study the module, then save or print notes.

Open the lesson when available, follow module-wise explanations and diagrams, and use Print / Save PDF for offline revision.

Technical terms Englishലും supporting explanation Malayalamലും ലഭ്യമായേക്കാം.

Step 5 · Practise

Use questions and mock exams after studying.

Attempt available quizzes and mock papers honestly. Use feedback to identify topics that need another revision, not as an official examination record.

Scoreനേക്കാൾ തെറ്റായ answers മനസ്സിലാക്കുന്നതിന് മുൻഗണന നൽകുക.

Step 6 · Guidance

Ask for guidance or report a problem.

Use Ask POLY AI to find pages and understand general study topics. Use Help when a code, title, lesson, note or link is missing or wrong.

Correction reportൽ revision, subject code, page URL, screenshot എന്നിവ ചേർക്കുക.

Our mission is clarity, not content confusion.

POLY PMNA organises study support around curriculum, programme, semester and subject code. The goal is to reduce unnecessary searching and help students reach the right academic resource faster.

  • Keep Revision 2026, Revision 2021 and the 2015 archive distinct.
  • Make lessons readable on phone, desktop and printable PDF.
  • Improve the portal when students report missing or incorrect material.
◎

What students should always verify.

POLY PMNA is an independent study-support platform. It does not replace official SITTTR documents, institution instructions, approved textbooks, teachers or examination notifications.

  • Confirm the revision, subject code and syllabus.
  • Confirm marks, duration and examination pattern from official notices.
  • Treat AI explanations and mock scores as study guidance, not official decisions.
